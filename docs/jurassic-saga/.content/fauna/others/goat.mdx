# Goat

The goat is a domesticated herbivorous mammal found throughout the world.  
Known for their sure-footedness and curious nature, goats have been companions to humans for thousands of years.  
In-game, the goat is a passive mob that can be bred and serves as a food source.

---

## Appearance
- Medium-sized mammal with a stocky build
- Short fur with various color patterns
- Distinctive horns (varies by breed)
- Short tail and hooved feet
- In-game size: **2 blocks long** and **1 block tall**

---

## Behavior
- **Passive**: Does not attack players or other mobs
- Wanders peacefully in plains and mountainous areas
- Can be bred using wheat
- May jump or climb when exploring terrain

---

## Obtaining
Goat spawns naturally in the overworld.

---

## Drops
When defeated, Goat may drop:  
- ![](@jurassic-saga:textures/item/natural/entity/animal/v1/extant/goat/goat_raw) [**Goat Raw Meat**](@jurassic-saga:meats)

## Trivia
- Goats in Jurassic Saga reference their iconic role as dinosaur bait throughout the Jurassic Park franchise
- The different variants (JPOG, JWE, JW, JP) pay homage to goats' appearances across various Jurassic Park games and films
- In *Jurassic Park* (1993), a goat was famously used as live bait to lure the Tyrannosaurus rex during the paddock tour
