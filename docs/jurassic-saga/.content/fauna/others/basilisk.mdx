# Basiliscus amoratus

The Basiliscus amoratus, commonly known as the **brown basilisk**, is a species of lizard native to **Central America**.  
It is famous for its ability to run across the surface of water, earning it the nickname “Jesus Christ lizard.”  
In‑game, the Basilisk is a small, passive mob that adds atmosphere to tropical and river environments.

---

## Appearance
- Slender lizard with a long tail and agile limbs.  
- Distinctive crest along the back and head, used for display.  
- Brown‑green coloration that blends into forest and riverside foliage.  
- Adults reach up to **2 blocks in length** and **1 block in height** in‑game.  

---

## Behavior
- **Passive**:  
  - Flees quickly when approached, often running across water surfaces.  
  - Frequently basks on rocks, logs, or riverbanks.  
  - Avoids predators and players, relying on speed and agility.  

---

## Health
- **Adult**: 18 health (x9 hearts)
