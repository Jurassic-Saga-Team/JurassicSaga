# Hadrosaurus foulkii

Hadrosaurus was a duck-billed herbivore from the **Late Cretaceous period** of North America.
Known for its strong hind legs and broad, flattened snout, it likely moved in herds and may have used vocalizations to communicate.
As one of the earliest hadrosaurids described, Hadrosaurus represents a key member of the "duck-billed" dinosaurs.

---

## Appearance
- Large, robust hadrosaur with a long tail and powerful hind limbs
- Broad, flat beak used for cropping vegetation
- Smooth, rounded body shape typical of early hadrosaurids
- In-game size: **11 blocks long** and **4 blocks tall**

---

## Behavior
- **Neutral**: Will not attack unless provoked
- Retaliates when attacked
- Often found in herds, reflecting real-world social behavior
- Uses vocalizations to alert nearby herd members of danger

---

## Obtaining
Hadrosaurus does not spawn naturally. To obtain one, you must:
1. Find ![](@jurassic-saga:textures/item/natural/entity/animal/v1/extinct/hadrosaurus/hadrosaurus_fossil_remains) [**Hadrosaurus Remains**](@jurassic-saga:fossil) or fossils
2. Use the fossil revival process to create a Hadrosaurus
3. Hatch from an **Alligator Egg** type egg

---

## Diet
- **Herbivore**: feeds primarily on <img src="https://i.imgur.com/INKBvsq.gif" width={32} height={32} style={{imageRendering: "pixelated"}}/> [**Leaves**](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Leaves)
- Grazes peacefully in open plains and forest edges

## <img width={64} src="https://i.imgur.com/DTOgOBL.png" alt="Mr.DNA"/> Mr. DNA's Fun Trivia
- Hadrosaurus is the type species of the Hadrosauridae family, a family of ornithiscian dinosaurs including Parasaurolophus and Corythosaurus!
- Hadrosaurus was the first dinosaur skeleton to have been discovered in the United States, with it's discovery site now being a national historic landmark!
- Hadrosaurus' holotype was discovered in marine sediments, suggesting that it's skeleton was transported by a river and later deposited in the Cretaceous sea!
