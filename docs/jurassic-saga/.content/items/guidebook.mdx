# Guidebook

The Guidebook is an essential tool for learning about creatures, their genetics, and their needs.  
It provides detailed information on each species, including gene variants, diets, and real-time stats, serving as your complete in-game encyclopedia for Jurassic Saga.

---

## Functionality

- **Type**: Information and inspection tool
- **Primary Uses**:
  - View detailed species information
  - Inspect creature stats in real-time
  - Reference genetic variants
  - Learn about diets and creature needs

---

## Usage

### Reading Species Information
- Open the Guidebook to access detailed data about all discovered species
- Browse information on diet, behavior, size, and genetics
- Acts as an in-game encyclopedia for Jurassic Saga

### Inspecting Creatures
Right-click any dinosaur or creature with the Guidebook to view real-time stats:
- **Health**: Current health points
- **Hunger**: Current hunger level
- **Thirst**: Current thirst level
- **Growth Stage**: Baby, juvenile, or adult
- **Mood/Stress**: Emotional state (if applicable)

### Genetic Reference
- Lists all possible gene variants for each species
- Shows cosmetic genes (Arid, Pasture, Ocean, Taiga, etc.)
- Displays functional genes and their effects
- Helps players understand how genetics affect appearance, stats, and behavior

For more information on genes, see the [**Genes**](@jurassic-saga:genes) page.

## Trivia
- The Guidebook is inspired by the various field guides and databases used by paleontologists and park staff in the Jurassic Park franchise
- The real-time creature inspection feature allows players to monitor their dinosaurs' welfare, similar to how park rangers would track animal health in the films
- The Guidebook serves as a portable database, making it easy to reference creature information while exploring your park
