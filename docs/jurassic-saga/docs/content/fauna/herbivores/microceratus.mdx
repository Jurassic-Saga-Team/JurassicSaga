# Microceratus gobiensis

*Microceratus* (meaning **"small-horned"**) is a small ceratopsian dinosaur from the Cretaceous period of Mongolia.
It walked on two legs, had short forelimbs, a small frill, and a beak-like mouth typical of early ceratopsians.
Despite its size, it was agile and well-adapted to forested environments.

---

## Appearance
- Small, lightweight ceratopsian with a short frill
- Beaked mouth used for cropping vegetation
- Long tail aiding balance while running
- In-game size: **1 block long** and **1 block tall**

---

## Behavior
- **Neutral (Requires 2)**: A single Microceratus will flee when threatened; when **two or more** are present, they may stand their ground and retaliate if attacked
- Often found in small groups, darting between vegetation for cover

---

## Obtaining
Microceratus does not spawn naturally. To obtain one, you must:
1. Find ![](@jurassic-saga:textures/item/natural/entity/animal/v2/extinct/microceratus/microceratus_fossil_remains) [**Microceratus Remains**]($content/items/fossil) or fossils
2. Use the fossil revival process to create a Microceratus
3. Hatch from a **Basilisk Egg** type egg

---

## Diet
- **Herbivore**: feeds primarily on <img src="https://i.imgur.com/INKBvsq.gif" width={32} height={32} style={{imageRendering: "pixelated"}}/> [**Leaves**](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Leaves)
- Grazes on low vegetation and shrubs

---

## Drops
When defeated, Microceratus may drop:
- ![](@jurassic-saga:textures/item/natural/entity/animal/v2/extinct/microceratus/microceratus_fossil_remains) [**Microceratus Remains**]($content/items/fossil)
- ![](@jurassic-saga:textures/item/natural/entity/animal/v2/extinct/microceratus/microceratus_raw) [**Microceratus Raw Meat**]($content/items/meats)

## <img width={64} src="https://i.imgur.com/DTOgOBL.png" alt="Mr.DNA"/> Mr. DNA's Fun Trivia
- The achievement "Please Do Not Litter" is likely a playful reference to Microceratus's small size and the importance of noticing small creatures in their environment.
- Despite the name *Microceratus* meaning "small-horned," this dinosaur actually had no horns  only a small frill.
