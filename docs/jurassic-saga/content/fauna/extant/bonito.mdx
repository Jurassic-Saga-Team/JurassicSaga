# Bonito

The *Bonito* is a fast-swimming, predatory fish found in warm ocean waters worldwide.
Part of the tuna family, bonitos are known for their streamlined bodies and speed.
In-game, the bonito is a passive aquatic mob that adds life to ocean environments.

---

## Appearance
- Streamlined, torpedo-shaped body built for speed
- Dark blue-gray back with silvery-white belly
- Distinctive oblique stripes along the upper body
- Forked tail fin for powerful swimming
- In-game size: **2 blocks long** and **1 block tall**

---

## Behavior
- **Passive**: Does not attack players or other mobs
- Swims rapidly through open ocean waters
- Often found in schools with other fish
- Avoids predators with quick bursts of speed

---

## Obtaining
Bonito spawns naturally in ocean biomes.

---

## Diet
- **Carnivore**: feeds on small fish and invertebrates
- Hunts prey in open water using speed and agility

---
