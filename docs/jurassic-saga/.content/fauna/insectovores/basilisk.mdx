# Basilisk

The Basilisk is a legendary reptile often described in folklore as a serpent or lizard with deadly powers.  
In‑game, the Basilisk is a small, passive mob that does not attack players, serving more as a curiosity than a threat.  
Its mythical origins make it a unique addition to the Jurassic Saga bestiary.

---

## Appearance
- Small, serpent‑like body with scaled skin.  
- Bright, piercing eyes often associated with its mythical powers.  
- In‑game size: up to **2 blocks in length** and **1 block in height**.  

---

- Wanders slowly, reflecting its role as a mythical creature rather than a predator.
