# Cearadactylus atrox

Cearadactylus was a pterosaur from the **Early Cretaceous period**, discovered in Brazil.  
It had a long, toothed beak adapted for catching fish, making it a specialized piscivore.  
With a wingspan of around **4–5 meters**, it was a capable flyer that likely soared over coastal and river environments.

---

## Appearance
- Long, narrow skull with sharp teeth suited for gripping fish.  
- Large wings with membranous structure, typical of pterosaurs.  
- Lightweight body adapted for flight.  
- In‑game size: up to **3 blocks in length** and **2 blocks in height**.  

---

## Behavior
- **Passive**:  
    - Flees when attacked, taking to the air if possible.  
    - Often seen gliding above water sources in search of fish.  
- Will perch on rocks or trees when resting.  
- Avoids predators and large carnivores.  

---

## Diet
- **Piscivore**  
- Feeds primarily on:  
    - ![](@jurassic-saga:gifs/piscivore_edible.gif) fishes  
- In‑game, it swoops down to catch fish from rivers, lakes, and coastal areas.  

---

## Health
- **Adult**: 60 health (x30 hearts)
