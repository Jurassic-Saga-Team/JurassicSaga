# Parasaurolophus cyrtocristatus

A herbivorous dinosaur from the **Late Cretaceous**, known for its long, backward-curving crest.  
The crest may have been used for **visual display**, **sound resonance**, or **thermoregulation**.  
Parasaurolophus likely lived in herds and fed on a variety of vegetation.

---

## Appearance
- Large hadrosaur with a long, sweeping cranial crest
- Slender body with strong hind limbs and a balancing tail
- Smooth, rounded snout typical of hadrosaurids
- In-game size: **12 blocks long** and **5 blocks tall**

---

## Behavior
- **Neutral**: Will not attack unless provoked
- Retaliates when attacked
- Often found in herds, using group movement for safety
- Uses its crest to produce low-frequency calls that alert nearby herd members

---

## Obtaining
Parasaurolophus does not spawn naturally. To obtain one, you must:
1. Find ![](@jurassic-saga:textures/item/natural/entity/animal/v1/extinct/parasaurolophus/parasaurolophus_fossil_remains) [**Parasaurolophus Remains**](@jurassic-saga:fossil) or fossils
2. Use the fossil revival process to create a Parasaurolophus
3. Hatch from an **Ostrich Egg** type egg

---

## Diet
- **Herbivore**: feeds primarily on <img src="https://i.imgur.com/INKBvsq.gif" width={32} height={32} style={{imageRendering: "pixelated"}}/> [**Leaves**](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Leaves)
- Grazes peacefully in open plains and forest edges

---

## Drops
When defeated, Parasaurolophus may drop:

## Trivia
- The achievement "The One With A Pompadour" is a playful reference to Parasaurolophus's distinctive backward-curving crest, which resembles a pompadour hairstyle
- Parasaurolophus's hollow crest could produce distinctive trumpeting sounds, making it one of the few dinosaurs that scientists believe could vocalize in unique ways
