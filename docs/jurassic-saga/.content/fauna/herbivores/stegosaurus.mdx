# Stegosaurus stenops

Stegosaurus was a large, plated herbivore from the **Late Jurassic period**, instantly recognizable by its distinctive double row of bony plates along its back.  
It had a small head, a bulky body, and a spiked tail (thagomizer) that may have been used for defense.  
Fossils of Stegosaurus have been found in **North America and Europe**, making it one of the most iconic dinosaurs of its time.

---

## Appearance
- Double row of large, upright plates along its back.  
- Small head with a beak‑like mouth adapted for cropping plants.  
- Strong, stocky legs supporting its heavy body.  
- Tail tipped with four long spikes (thagomizer).  
- In‑game size: up to **14 blocks in length** and **7 blocks in height**.  

---

## Behavior
- **Neutral**: Stegosaurus does not attack players unless provoked.  
- Moves slowly in herds, grazing on vegetation.  
- May display defensive behavior with its tail spikes when threatened.  

---

## Diet
- **Herbivore** — feeds primarily on [Leaves](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Leaves).  
- Browses on low‑lying plants and shrubs.
