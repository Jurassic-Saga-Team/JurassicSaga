# Euoplocephalus tutus

*Euoplocephalus* is a genus of large herbivorous ankylosaurid dinosaur from the **Late Cretaceous** of Canada.  
It is one of the best-known ankylosaurs, famous for its heavy armor and powerful tail club.

---

## Appearance
- Thick, low-slung body covered in bony osteoderms
- Large shoulder spikes providing additional defense
- Heavy, muscular tail ending in a distinctive **tail club**
- Broad, low head with a beak suited for cropping vegetation
- In-game size: **7 blocks long** and **2 blocks tall**

---

## Behavior
- **Neutral (Requires 2)**: A single Euoplocephalus remains calm and will not attack; when **two or more** are present, they become defensive and will retaliate if provoked
- Slow-moving but extremely durable
- Uses its tail club to deliver powerful defensive strikes
- Prefers forested or semi-arid environments, often seen grazing peacefully

---

## Obtaining
Euoplocephalus does not spawn naturally. To obtain one, you must:
1. Find ![](@jurassic-saga:textures/item/natural/entity/animal/v2/extinct/euoplocephalus/euoplocephalus_fossil_remains) [**Euoplocephalus Remains**](@jurassic-saga:fossil) or fossils
2. Use the fossil revival process to create a Euoplocephalus
3. Hatch from an **Ostrich Egg** type egg

---

## Diet
- **Herbivore**: feeds primarily on <img src="https://i.imgur.com/INKBvsq.gif" width={32} height={32} style={{imageRendering: "pixelated"}}/> [**Leaves**](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Leaves)
- Grazes on low vegetation and shrubs

---

## <img width={64} src="https://i.imgur.com/DTOgOBL.png" alt="Mr.DNA"/> Mr. DNA's Fun Trivia
- Euoplocephalus was originally named "Stereocephalus", however that name had already been given to an insect!
- Euplocephalus was one of the largest Ankylosaurids, with it's size only being exceeded by Ankylosaurus itself, and possibly Tarchia and Cedarpelta!
- Euoplocephalus had a broad muzzle, indicating that it was possibly a non-selective feeder, perhaps similar to a hippopotamus!
