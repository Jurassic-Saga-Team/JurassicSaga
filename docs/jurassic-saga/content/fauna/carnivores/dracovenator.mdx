# Dracovenator regenti

*Dracovenator*, meaning "dragon hunter," is a genus of early theropod dinosaur from the **Early Jurassic** of South Africa.  
Closely related to Dilophosaurus, Dracovenator was a medium-sized predator that lived approximately 201–199 million years ago. It represents one of the earliest large predatory dinosaurs in Africa.

---

## Appearance
- Medium-sized theropod with a robust build
- Elongated skull with sharp, serrated teeth
- Strong legs built for pursuit hunting
- Relatively long arms compared to later theropods
- Likely had crests or ridges on its skull, similar to its relative Dilophosaurus
- In-game size: **8 blocks long** and **3 blocks tall**

---

## Behavior
- **Hostile**: Actively hunts players and most creatures on sight
- Aggressive predator that pursues prey relentlessly
- Medium-sized hunter capable of taking down various targets

---

## Obtaining
Dracovenator does not spawn naturally. To obtain one, you must:
1. Enable [Experimental Features]($references/experimental)
2. Find ![](@jurassic-saga:textures/item/natural/entity/animal/v2/extinct/dracovenator/dracovenator_fossil_remains) [**Dracovenator Remains**](@jurassic-saga:fossil) or fossils
3. Use the fossil revival process to create a Dracovenator
4. Hatch from an **Alligator Egg** type egg

## <img width={64} src="https://i.imgur.com/DTOgOBL.png" alt="Mr.DNA"/> Mr. DNA's Fun Trivia
- Dracovenator has close relation with Dilophosaurus, being a member of Dilophosauridae, alongside Dilophosaurus, of course!
- Dracovenator is only known from very fragmentary remains and a juvenile's snout, making it quite enigmatic compared to it's cousin Dilophosaurus!
- Dracovenator was discovered in 1981 by James Kitching, in South Africa. It's name has an interesting origin, while by itself it means "dragon hunter" it also refers to its discovery in the foothills of Drakensberg, which is "Dragon's Mountain" in the Dutch language, while "regenti" is named after Professor Kitching's assistant: Regent 'Lucas' Huma.
- Dracovenator's appearance in Jurassic Saga is based on the 1990 novel depiction of Dilophosaurus.
- During the development of the mod, Dracovenator used to use Sinosaurus's name.
- The Rainforest and Ocean cosmetic skins are based respectively on Primal Carnage and Dino D-Day.
