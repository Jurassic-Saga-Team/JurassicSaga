# Dryosaurus altus

Dryosaurus was a small, swift ornithopod from the **Late Jurassic period**.
Its name means *“oak lizard”*, referencing the forested environments it inhabited.
Dryosaurus relied on speed and agility to avoid predators, living in groups and feeding on low‑lying vegetation.

---

## Appearance
- Slender body built for speed and agility.
- Long tail used for balance while running.
- Small head with beak‑like mouth adapted for cropping plants.
- In‑game size: up to **3 blocks in length** and **1 block in height** (with the model extending slightly into the second block).

---

## Behavior
- **Passive**: Dryosaurus does not attack players.
- Moves quickly and nervously, reflecting its real‑world reliance on speed.
- Often found in small groups, grazing together.

---

## Diet
- **Herbivore**: feeds primarily on <img src="https://i.imgur.com/INKBvsq.gif" width={32} height={32} style={{imageRendering: "pixelated"}}/> [**Leaves**](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Leaves).
- Prefers low‑lying plants and foliage.

## <img width={64} src="https://i.imgur.com/DTOgOBL.png" alt="Mr.DNA"/> Mr. DNA's Fun Trivia
- A fossil of a Dryosaurus hatchling confirmed that Dryosaurus followed similar patterns of craniofacial development to other vertebrates, with it's eyes being proportionally large while young and the muzzle proportionally short!
- Dryosaurus fed on low growing vegetation in the floodplains of the Late Jurassic, in the Morrison Formation!
- Dryosaurus lived among giant animals such as Apatosaurus, Brachiosaurus and Diplodocus!
