# Callovosaurus leedsi

*Callovosaurus* (meaning **“Callovian lizard”**) is a genus of iguanodontian dinosaur known from Middle Jurassic rocks of England.
It is one of the earliest known dryosaurids and represents a small, agile herbivore that lived in forested environments.

---

## Appearance
- Small, lightweight ornithopod with a slender build.
- Long tail used for balance while running.
- Narrow head with a beak suited for cropping vegetation.
- Adults reach up to **4 blocks in length** and **1 block in height** in‑game.

---

## Behavior
- **Passive**: Will flee when attacked or startled.
- Often found grazing in small groups. 
- Prefers open forests and lightly wooded areas.

---

## Diet
- **Herbivore**: feeds primarily on <img src="https://i.imgur.com/INKBvsq.gif" width={32} height={32} style={{imageRendering: "pixelated"}}/> [**Leaves**](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Leaves).  
- In‑game, it wanders and grazes peacefully.

---
