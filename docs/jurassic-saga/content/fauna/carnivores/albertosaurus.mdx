# Albertosaurus sarcophagus
 
*Albertosaurus*, meaning **"Alberta lizard,"** is a genus of large tyrannosaurid theropod dinosaur from the **Late Cretaceous** of western North America.
It was a fast, lightly built relative of *Tyrannosaurus*, equipped with a powerful bite and strong legs suited for active predation.
 
---
 
## Appearance
- Large, bipedal tyrannosaurid with a deep skull and powerful jaws
- Tiny two-fingered forelimbs, typical of tyrannosaurs
- Long, slender hind limbs built for speed
- In-game size: **15 blocks long** and **5 blocks tall**
---
 
## Behavior
- **Hostile**: Albertosaurus will attack players and mobs
- Fast and dangerous, with a powerful bite
- Often found roaming open terrain
---
 
## Obtaining
Albertosaurus does not spawn naturally. To obtain one, you must:
1. Find ![](@jurassic-saga:textures/item/natural/entity/animal/v2/extinct/albertosaurus/albertosaurus_fossil_remains) [**Albertosaurus Remains**]($content/items/fossil) or fossils
2. Use the fossil revival process to create an Albertosaurus
3. Hatch from an **Ostrich Egg** type egg
---
 
## Diet
- **Carnivore**: feeds on <img src="https://i.imgur.com/4K6xsJA.gif" width={32} height={32} style={{imageRendering: "pixelated"}}/> meat sources
- Opportunistic predator, may scavenge when hunting is not possible
---
 
## Drops
When defeated, Albertosaurus may drop:
- ![](@jurassic-saga:textures/item/natural/entity/animal/v2/extinct/albertosaurus/albertosaurus_fossil_remains) [**Albertosaurus Remains**]($content/items/fossil)
- ![](@jurassic-saga:textures/item/natural/entity/animal/v2/extinct/albertosaurus/albertosaurus_raw) [**Albertosaurus Raw Meat**]($content/items/meats)

## <img width={64} src="https://i.imgur.com/DTOgOBL.png" alt="Mr.DNA"/> Mr. DNA's Fun Trivia
- Albertosaurus lived a few million years before its mighty cousin, the Tyrannosaurus, in what is now the Canadian province of Alberta!
- Albertosaurus was named after Alberta, a Canadian province that was, funnily enough, established in the very same year the dinosaur was officially named: 1905!
- Albertosaurus might have been a pack hunter, as fossils of multiple individuals have been found together, although this may have also just been caused by other circumstances and not because they were pack hunters!
