# Mussaurus patagonicus

*Mussaurus* is a genus of herbivorous sauropodomorph dinosaur that lived in southern Argentina during the **Early Jurassic**, specifically the Sinemurian stage.
Despite its name meaning “mouse lizard,” adults were medium‑sized dinosaurs, while juveniles were famously tiny.

---

## Appearance
- Small to medium‑sized sauropodomorph with a long neck and sturdy limbs.
- Juveniles are notably tiny compared to adults, reflecting the species’ famous growth difference.
- Adults have a more robust build with a deep chest and strong hind legs.
- Skin patterns vary depending on cosmetic genes.
- Adults reach up to **8 blocks in length** and **3 blocks in height** in‑game.  

---

## Behavior
- **Neutral**: 
    - Will not attack unless provoked.
    - Flees from large predators or sudden threats.
- Often found in small family groups or herds.
- Juveniles may follow adults for protection.
- Occasionally rears up to reach higher vegetation.

---

## Diet
- **Herbivore**: feeds primarily on ![](@jurassic-saga:gifs/leaves.gif) [**Leaves**](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Leaves).  
- May also graze on low shrubs and other plant blocks.
