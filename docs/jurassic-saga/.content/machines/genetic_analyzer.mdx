# Genetic Analyzer

The Genetic Analyzer is an early‑stage machine used to extract DNA from biological samples.  
It is the first step in the genetic engineering process and is required before synthesizing embryos or applying genetic modifications.

---

## Information

- Processes biological samples into **DNA Fragments** or **Full DNA**  
- Accepts fossils and remains  
- Processing time varies based on sample quality  
- Does not require fuel  
- Some samples may fail and produce degraded material  

---

## Usage

1. Insert a biological sample into the input slot  
2. The analyzer begins processing automatically  
3. Once complete, collect the DNA from the output slot  
4. DNA can then be used in the **DNA Synthesizer** or other machines  

### Supported Inputs
- Fossils  
- Remains
- Ambers
- Deposits


### Outputs
- DNA Fragment  
- Full DNA  
- Gravel
- Dirt
- Coal
- Sand
