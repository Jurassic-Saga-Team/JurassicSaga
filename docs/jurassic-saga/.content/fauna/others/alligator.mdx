# Alligator

The alligator is a large, semi‑aquatic reptile native to **North America**.  
Known for its powerful jaws and armored body, it thrives in rivers, swamps, and wetlands.  
In‑game, the alligator is a hostile mob that can pose a danger to players who wander too close to water.

---

## Appearance
- Long, muscular body covered in tough scales.  
- Broad snout with sharp teeth.  
- Short legs with webbed feet for swimming.  
- Adults reach up to **7 blocks in length** and **1 block in height** in‑game.  

---

## Behavior
- **Hostile**: 
- Attacks players and other mobs when approached.  
- Prefers water environments but can move on land.  
- May lurk near rivers or swamps, ambushing prey.  

---

## Health
- **Adult**: 18 health (x9 hearts)
