# Alligator

The alligator is a large, semi-aquatic reptile native to **North America**.  
Known for its powerful jaws and armored body, it thrives in rivers, swamps, and wetlands.  
In-game, the alligator is a hostile mob that can pose a danger to players who wander too close to water.

---

## Appearance
- Long, muscular body covered in tough scales
- Broad snout with sharp teeth
- Short legs with webbed feet for swimming
- In-game size: **7 blocks long** and **1 block tall**

---

## Behavior
- **Hostile**: Attacks players and other mobs when approached
- Prefers water environments but can move on land
- May lurk near rivers or swamps, ambushing prey

---

## Obtaining
Alligator spawns naturally in swamps near Witch Huts.

---

## Drops
When defeated, Alligator may drop:

## Trivia
- Alligators are one of the few modern animals included in Jurassic Saga, serving as a source of Alligator Eggs needed for hatching medium-sized dinosaurs
- Real-world alligators have remained virtually unchanged for millions of years, making them living fossils alongside dinosaurs in the mod
