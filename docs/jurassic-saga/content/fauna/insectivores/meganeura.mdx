# Meganeura monyi 
Meganeura was a giant predatory insect from the **Carboniferous period**, with fossils discovered in Europe and North America. 
It resembled a modern dragonfly but was far larger, with a wingspan of up to **70 cm**. 
Its enormous size was made possible by the high-oxygen atmosphere of the Carboniferous, which allowed insects to grow much larger than today. 

---

## Appearance
- Large dragonfly-like body with elongated wings
- Compound eyes for detecting movement
- Long legs adapted for grasping prey
- In-game size: **2 blocks long** and **1 block tall**
    
---

## Behavior
- **Passive**: Does not attack players or mobs
- Flies slowly around swamps, forests, or open areas
- Adds atmosphere and immersion to prehistoric environments

---

## Obtaining
Meganeura does not spawn naturally. To obtain one, you must:
1. Find [**Meganeura Remains**](@jurassic-saga:remains) or fossils
2. Use the fossil revival process to create a Meganeura
3. Hatch from a **Spider Egg** type egg

---

## Diet 
- **Insectivore**: feeds on <img src="https://i.imgur.com/m2zd5FJ.gif" width={32} height={32} style={{imageRendering: "pixelated"}}/> smaller insects and invertebrates

---

## Drops
When defeated, Meganeura may drop:
- [**Meganeura Remains**](@jurassic-saga:remains)
- [**Meganeura Raw Meat**](@jurassic-saga:raw_meat)

## <img width={64} src="https://i.imgur.com/DTOgOBL.png" alt="Mr.DNA"/> Mr. DNA's Fun Trivia
- The achievement "Look At The Size Of It" references the massive wingspan of Meganeura compared to modern dragonflies!
- Meganeura lived approximately 300 million years ago, long before dinosaurs appeared!
- The Carboniferous atmosphere contained up to 35% oxygen (compared to today's 21%), which enabled insects to grow to enormous sizes!
- Meganeura was one of the biggest insects to ever fly, being rivaled only by its slightly bigger cousin from the Permian period, Meganeuropsis!
- Meganeura is part of a family of extinct insects called Meganisoptera, also commonly known as the Griffinflies, that also frequently get incorrectly called Giant Dragonflies, although both Dragonflies and Griffinflies belong to the Superorder Odonatoptera!
- Meganeura lived in what is now the Commentry Shales Formation of France, and it lived alongside animals such as Arthropleura and Orthacanthus!
