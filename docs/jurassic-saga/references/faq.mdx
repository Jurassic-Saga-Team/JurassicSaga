# Frequently Asked Questions

Welcome to the Jurassic Saga FAQ! This page answers common questions about the mod, its features, development, and compatibility.

---

## About Jurassic Saga

### What is Jurassic Saga?

Jurassic Saga aims to bring creatures from across the entire Jurassic Park franchise into your Minecraft world, with a major focus on park building and genetic engineering.

The creatures, cosmetics, and blocks featured in this mod draw inspiration from:
- The Jurassic Park and Jurassic World films
- Michael Crichton's original novels
- Real-life toy lines (Kenner, Hasbro, Mattel)
- Various Jurassic Park/World video games (JPOG, JW:E, JW:TG, and more)

Our goal is to create an immersive prehistoric park-building experience that honors the entire franchise while adding original content where needed.

## Technical Requirements

### What Minecraft versions are supported?

Jurassic Saga is currently available for:
- **Minecraft 1.21.1** — Neoforge and Fabric
- **Minecraft 1.20.1** — Forge and Fabric

We are **not** planning to support versions older than 1.20.1.

### What mods are required?

Jurassic Saga requires the following dependencies:
- **[Azurelib](https://modrinth.com/mod/azurelib)** — Animation and model library
- **[Traveler's Tales](https://modrinth.com/mod/travelers-tales)** — Additional framework support

Make sure to install the correct versions of these dependencies for your Minecraft version.

### Is there a Bedrock Edition version?

**No.** There is **NOT** and will **NOT** be a Pocket Edition or Bedrock Edition version of Jurassic Saga, ever.

This is a Java Edition exclusive mod. Please do not ask about Bedrock support.

## Creature Design & Accuracy

### Will creatures be accurate to the source material?

We aim to keep creatures close to their original appearances in the films, books, and games, but they won't always be exact recreations.

**Design Philosophy:**
- **Film/Game Accuracy**: Creatures are based on their franchise appearances, not real paleontology
- **Consistency**: Some adjustments are made for visual consistency across the mod
- **Gameplay Needs**: Designs may be adapted for better Minecraft gameplay
- **Original Concepts**: We include original designs for underrepresented species or those lacking official visuals

### Why doesn't [creature] look exactly like the movie?

Some adjustments are necessary for:
- Minecraft's blocky aesthetic and technical limitations
- Maintaining consistent art style across all creatures
- Balancing gameplay and visual fidelity
- Filling gaps where official designs don't exist or are inconsistent

## Creature Behavior & Features

### Do animals breed currently?

Animals don't breed in the mod yet. Breeding mechanics are planned for a future update.

Currently, all creatures are obtained through the genetic engineering system (DNA extraction, modification, and incubation).

### Can animals be tamed?

No, animals will **not** be tameable in the mod.

Jurassic Saga focuses on park management and containment rather than traditional Minecraft pet mechanics. Creatures remain wild and must be managed through enclosures, feeding systems, and security measures.

### I saw people playing with animals not in my version. How do I get them?

Some creatures are available early through **Experimental Features**. These creatures have basic AI and are subject to changes.

To enable experimental features:
1. Access your mod settings
2. Enable "Experimental Features" in the Jurassic Saga configuration
3. Restart your game

For detailed instructions, see [**Experimental Features**]($references/experimental).

**Note:** Experimental creatures may have bugs, incomplete features, or placeholder behavior.

## Additional Questions

### What's the difference between Jurassic Saga and other dinosaur mods?

Jurassic Saga focuses specifically on the **Jurassic Park franchise** rather than real paleontology. Our creatures, aesthetics, and gameplay are designed around park building and franchise authenticity.

### How often is the mod updated?

Updates are released when features are complete and stable. Follow our [Discord server](https://discord.gg/3b8k78f) for development progress and release announcements.

### Can I use Jurassic Saga in my modpack?

Yes! Jurassic Saga can be included in modpacks. Please credit the development team and link back to the official mod page.
