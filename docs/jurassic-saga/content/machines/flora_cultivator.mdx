# Flora Cultivator

The Flora Cultivator is a specialized machine used to grow prehistoric plants from Plant DNA.  
It provides a controlled environment that allows ancient plant species to develop and thrive in the modern world.

---

## Functionality

- **Type**: Plant cultivation machine
- **Processes**: Plant DNA into fully grown prehistoric plants
- **Capacity**: 3 plants simultaneously
- **Cultivation Time**: Varies based on plant species
- **Automation**: Automatically maintains ideal growing conditions

---

## Usage

1. Insert Plant DNA into an input slot (up to 3 samples)
2. The cultivator begins growing the plant automatically
3. Once complete, collect the grown plant from the output slot
4. The plant can then be placed in the world or used for other purposes

---

## Accepted Inputs

The Flora Cultivator accepts:
- Plant DNA (extracted from fossils and deposits)

---

## Outputs

- Fully grown prehistoric plants ready to be placed or used

## Trivia
- The Flora Cultivator allows players to recreate prehistoric ecosystems by growing ancient plant species alongside dinosaurs
- Unlike the Incubator, the Flora Cultivator has a capacity of 3 slots instead of 5, reflecting the different growth requirements of plants
- The Flora Cultivator operates without requiring any fuel or power source
- Growing prehistoric plants can help create authentic Jurassic environments and provide food sources for herbivorous creatures
