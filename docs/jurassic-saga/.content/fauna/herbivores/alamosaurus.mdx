# Alamosaurus sanjuanensis

*Alamosaurus*, meaning **"Ojo Alamo lizard,"** is a genus of titanosaurian sauropod dinosaur containing a single known species, *Alamosaurus sanjuanensis*. 
It lived during the **Late Cretaceous** and was one of the last surviving sauropods in North America.

---

## Appearance
- Massive titanosaur with a long neck and powerful tail
- Thick, column-like legs supporting its enormous weight
- Lightly armored skin texture, typical of titanosaurs
- In-game size: **28 blocks long**, **17 blocks tall**, making it one of the largest creatures in Jurassic Saga

---

## Behavior
- **Neutral**: Will not attack unless provoked
- If attacked, it retaliates with powerful attacks
- Slow-moving but extremely strong
- Often found roaming open plains or semi-arid environments in your park

---

## Obtaining
Alamosaurus does not spawn naturally. To obtain one, you must:
1. Find ![](@jurassic-saga:textures/item/natural/entity/animal/v2/extinct/alamosaurus/alamosaurus_fossil_remains) [**Alamosaurus Remains**](@jurassic-saga:fossil) or fossils
2. Use the fossil revival process to create an Alamosaurus
3. Hatch from an **Ostrich Egg** type egg

---

## Diet
- **Herbivore**: feeds primarily on ![](@jurassic-saga:gifs/leaves.gif) [**Leaves**](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Leaves)
- Feeds on large vegetation, shrubs, and tree foliage

---

## Drops
When defeated, Alamosaurus may drop:  
- ![](@jurassic-saga:textures/item/natural/entity/animal/v2/extinct/alamosaurus/alamosaurus_raw) [**Alamosaurus Raw Meat**](@jurassic-saga:meats)
- ![](@jurassic-saga:textures/item/natural/entity/animal/v2/extinct/alamosaurus/alamosaurus_fossil_remains) [**Alamosaurus Remains**](@jurassic-saga:fossil)
