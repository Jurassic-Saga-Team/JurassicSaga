# Scientist

The Scientist is a custom villager profession added by Jurassic Saga, specializing in genetic engineering equipment and rare cosmetic genes.
These lab-coated experts are essential for obtaining hard-to-find genes and genetic engineering supplies without extensive resource gathering.

---

## Appearance

- White lab coat with blue wrists
- Small green dot on the bottom right of the coat's front
- Lab coat is open all the way down
- Professional scientific appearance distinct from vanilla villager professions

---

## Job Site Block

Scientists require one of the following machines as their job site block:
- [**Flora Cultivator**](@jurassic-saga:flora_cultivator)
- [**DNA Modifier**](@jurassic-saga:dna_modifier)
- [**Gene Isolator**](@jurassic-saga:gene_isolator)
- [**Genetic Analyzer**](@jurassic-saga:genetic_analyzer)
- [**Incubator**](@jurassic-saga:incubator)

Place any of these machines near an unemployed villager to convert them into a Scientist.

---

## Trading

Scientists offer trades that become more valuable as you level them up through trading.

### Level 1 (Novice)
**Available Trades:**
- [**Syringe**](@jurassic-saga:syringe) — Essential tool for gene extraction and egg fertilization
- **Empty Test Tube** — Basic laboratory equipment

**Use Case:** Starting genetic engineering work and basic lab supplies.

---

### Level 2 (Apprentice)
**Available Trades:**
- **Basic Genes** — Common functional and cosmetic genes

**Use Case:** Obtaining genes without farming specific mobs.

---

### Level 3 (Journeyman)
**Available Trades:**
- **More Genes** — Expanded selection of functional and cosmetic genes
- **Rarer Gene Variants** — Less common genetic modifications

**Use Case:** Advanced genetic customization and creature modification.

---

### Level 4 (Expert)
**Available Trades:**
- **Chaos Effect Gene** — Exclusive cosmetic gene with unique appearance
- **Legacy Gene** — Classic cosmetic gene variant
- **Special Cosmetic Genes** — Rare and unique visual modifications

**Use Case:** Creating creatures with the rarest and most distinctive appearances.

---

### Level 5 (Master)
**Available Trades:**
- **Random DNA** — A random selected Species DNA, quality varies from 2% to 27%

**Use Case:** A easier way to obtain a certain species DNA.

---

## Finding Scientists

### Converting Villagers

1. **Obtain an Unemployed Villager**
   - Breed villagers or find naturally spawned unemployed villagers
   - Ensure the villager has not claimed a different job site block

2. **Place a Job Site Block**
   - Place one of the accepted machines ([Genetic Analyzer](@jurassic-saga:genetic_analyzer), [DNA Modifier](@jurassic-saga:dna_modifier), etc.) near the villager
   - The villager must be able to pathfind to the machine

3. **Confirmation**
   - The villager will walk to the machine and claim it as their job site
   - Their appearance will change to the Scientist outfit
   - They will begin offering Scientist trades

### Village Generation

Scientists may naturally generate in villages if genetic engineering machines are present during world generation (behavior may vary).

---

## Trading Tips

- **Level Up Efficiently**: Trade frequently to unlock higher-tier gene trades quickly
- **Multiple Scientists**: Create several Scientists to access different gene trades and refresh stock
- **Emerald Conservation**: Stock up on emeralds before attempting to purchase rare genes
- **Gene Comparison**: Compare Scientist trades with mob extraction using [**Syringes**](@jurassic-saga:syringe) to find the most efficient method
- **Chaos & Legacy Priority**: Save emeralds for Level 4 trades, as Chaos Effect and Legacy genes cannot be obtained any other way

---

## Strategy

### Early Game
Focus on Level 1 trades for **Syringes** to jumpstart your genetic engineering without crafting resources.

### Mid Game
Use Level 2-3 trades to supplement gene collection, especially for genes from hard-to-find mobs.

### Late Game
Invest in Level 4 trades for exclusive **Chaos Effect** and **Legacy** genes that define your creature collection's appearance.

---

## Trivia

- The Scientist profession is Jurassic Saga's equivalent to vanilla professions like Armorer or Toolsmith
- The white lab coat design is inspired by the geneticists and scientists throughout the Jurassic Park franchise
- Scientists are the only source of Chaos Effect and Legacy genes, making them essential for completionist players
- The green dot on the lab coat may represent an ID badge or security clearance marker
- Unlike vanilla villagers who work during the day, Scientists thematically represent 24/7 laboratory operations
