## Genetic Modifications

**Genes** are items that can be applied to prehistoric creatures to change appearance, behavior, stats, or biological traits.
Some genes are **cosmetic** only; others provide **functional** bonuses that affect combat, survival, or life cycle.
Not every gene works on every creature. Some genes are **stackable**, some are **species‑restricted**, and some are **unimplemented** or reserved for future updates.

---

## Categories

### Cosmetic Genes
Purely visual changes that alter a creature’s **texture**, **pattern**, or **coloration**.
Cosmetic genes do not change stats or behavior.

**Examples**
- **Arid Cosmetic** 
- **Pasture Cosmetic** 
- **Ocean Cosmetic** 
- **Taiga Cosmetic** 
- **Species‑specific cosmetics** such as **Chaos Effect**, **Forest**, etc.

---

### Functional Genes
Modify stats, abilities, or life cycle. See the list below for details and current status.

---

## Functional Genes

> Each entry lists the gene name, its effect, and whether it is currently implemented.

#### Lux Gene
- **Effect:** Adds a glow layer based on external lighting.
- **Status:** Unimplemented.

#### Toxic Skin
- **Effect:** When the creature is hit, the attacker receives **100 ticks of Wither**.

#### Venom Bite
- **Effect:** Creature melee attacks inflict **100 ticks of Wither**.

#### Aggressive Instinct
- **Effect:** Increases aggression by **0.25**.

#### Bone Density
- **Effect:** **+10 Armor Toughness**; **+10 Armor**.

#### Tooth Hardness
- **Effect:** **+8 Attack Damage**.

#### Skin Toughness
- **Effect:** **+5 Armor Toughness**; **+20 Armor**.

#### Light Skeleton
- **Effect:** **+0.25** movement speed.

#### Intensive Repair
- **Effect:** Increases natural regeneration from **1 heart** to **5 hearts**.

#### Macrosoma
- **Effect:** Increases model scale by **+0.1%** per application.
- **Stacking:** Up to **4** times.

#### Microsoma
- **Effect:** Decreases model scale by **−0.1%** per application.
- **Stacking:** Up to **4** times.

#### Gender Lean (Male / Female)
- **Effect:** Forces the creature to the specified gender.

#### Infertility
- **Effect:** Prevents the creature from breeding.

#### Lysine Deficiency
- **Effect:** Currently unused.

#### Frequent Ovulation
- **Effect:** Increases breeding frequency.

#### Parental Investment
- **Effect:** Babies grow faster when near their parents.

#### Immune Response
- **Effect:** No current use.

## Usage

- Apply genes using the **DNA Modifier** component.
- **Cosmetic genes** override the creature’s default texture immediately.
- **Functional genes** apply their stat or behavior changes immediately upon application.
- Some genes **stack** (for example, **Macrosoma** and **Microsoma**); stacking limits are noted above.
- **Genes are permanent** once applied and cannot be removed.

---

## Notes & Limitations

- **Species restrictions:** Some genes will not apply to certain creatures.
- **Unimplemented genes:** Genes marked unimplemented or unused have no effect until implemented.
- **Balance:** Applying multiple powerful genes can significantly change gameplay balance.
- **Irreversibility:** Genes cannot be removed after application.
