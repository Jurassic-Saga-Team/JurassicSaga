# Mussaurus patagonicus

*Mussaurus* is a genus of herbivorous sauropodomorph dinosaur that lived in southern Argentina during the **Early Jurassic**, specifically the Sinemurian stage.
Despite its name meaning "mouse lizard," adults were medium-sized dinosaurs, while juveniles were famously tiny.

---

## Appearance
- Small to medium-sized sauropodomorph with a long neck and sturdy limbs
- Juveniles are notably tiny compared to adults, reflecting the species' famous growth difference
- Adults have a more robust build with a deep chest and strong hind legs
- Skin patterns vary depending on cosmetic genes
- In-game size: **8 blocks long** and **3 blocks tall**

---

## Behavior
- **Neutral**: Will not attack unless provoked
- Flees from large predators or sudden threats
- Often found in small family groups or herds
- Juveniles may follow adults for protection
- Occasionally rears up to reach higher vegetation

---

## Obtaining
Mussaurus does not spawn naturally. To obtain one, you must:
1. Enable [Experimental Features]($references/experimental)
2. Find ![](@jurassic-saga:textures/item/natural/entity/animal/v4/extinct/mussaurus/mussaurus_fossil_remains) [**Mussaurus Remains**](@jurassic-saga:fossil) or fossils
3. Use the fossil revival process to create a Mussaurus
4. Hatch from an **Alligator Egg** type egg

---

## Diet
- **Herbivore**: feeds primarily on <img src="https://i.imgur.com/INKBvsq.gif" width={32} height={32} style={{imageRendering: "pixelated"}}/> [**Leaves**](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Leaves)
- May also graze on low shrubs and other plant blocks

## <img width={64} src="https://i.imgur.com/DTOgOBL.png" alt="Mr.DNA"/> Mr. DNA's Fun Trivia
- The name *Mussaurus* means "mouse lizard," which was based on the tiny size of the first fossils discovered—which turned out to be juveniles, not adults
- The achievement "Little Little Mussaurus" plays on the dinosaur's misleading name and the contrast between its tiny juveniles and medium-sized adults
