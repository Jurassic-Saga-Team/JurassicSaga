# Mesolimulus walchi

An ancient genus of horseshoe crab dating back to the Jurassic. It resembled modern horseshoe crabs and is an example of evolutionary stability. 
These creatures lived in shallow marine environments and fed on small invertebrates.

---

## Appearance
- Dome-shaped carapace similar to modern horseshoe crabs
- Long, pointed telson (tail spine) used for righting itself when flipped
- Multiple small legs and gill plates on the underside
- In-game size: **1 block long** and **1 block tall**

---

## Behavior
- **Passive**: Will not attack players or other mobs
- Slowly crawls along the seafloor or shallow waterbeds
- Burrows slightly into sand when resting or hiding
- Flees by scuttling away when disturbed

---

## Obtaining
Mesolimulus does not spawn naturally. To obtain one, you must:
1. Find ![](@jurassic-saga:textures/item/natural/entity/animal/v1/extinct/mesolimulus/mesolimulus_fossil_remains) [**Mesolimulus Remains**](@jurassic-saga:fossil) or fossils
2. Use the fossil revival process to create a Mesolimulus
3. Hatch from a **Spider Egg** type egg

---

## Drops
When defeated, Mesolimulus may drop:  
- ![](@jurassic-saga:textures/item/natural/entity/animal/v1/extinct/mesolimulus/mesolimulus_fossil_remains) [**Mesolimulus Remains**](@jurassic-saga:fossil)
- ![](@jurassic-saga:textures/item/natural/entity/animal/v1/extinct/mesolimulus/mesolimulus_raw) [**Mesolimulus Raw Meat**](@jurassic-saga:meats)

## Trivia
- The achievement "Living Fossil" references the fact that horseshoe crabs have remained virtually unchanged for hundreds of millions of years
- Mesolimulus is closely related to modern horseshoe crabs (Limulus), which are often called living fossils themselves
- Despite being called "crabs," horseshoe crabs are more closely related to spiders and scorpions than to true crabs
