# Troodon pectinodon

A small theropod from the **Late Cretaceous**, Troodon had one of the largest brain-to-body ratios among dinosaurs.  
Its keen senses, grasping hands, and serrated teeth suggest it was an intelligent and adaptable predator or omnivore.  
In-game, Troodon is fast, alert, and dangerous in groups.

---

## Appearance
- Small, lightly built theropod with long legs for speed
- Large eyes adapted for low-light hunting
- Narrow snout with finely serrated teeth
- In-game size: **3 blocks long** and **1 block tall**

---

## Behavior
- **Hostile**: Actively attacks players and small creatures on sight
- Hunts in coordinated packs, overwhelming prey with speed and numbers
- Uses quick darting movements and rapid bites to harass targets

---

## Obtaining
Troodon does not spawn naturally. To obtain one, you must:
1. Find ![](@jurassic-saga:textures/item/natural/entity/animal/v1/extinct/troodon/troodon_fossil_remains) [**Troodon Remains**](@jurassic-saga:fossil) or fossils
2. Use the fossil revival process to create a Troodon
3. Hatch from a **Chicken Egg** type egg

---

## Diet
- **Carnivore**: feeds on <img src="https://i.imgur.com/4K6xsJA.gif" width={32} height={32} style={{imageRendering: "pixelated"}}/> meat sources
- Hunts small to medium animals and scavenges opportunistically

---

## Drops
When defeated, Troodon may drop:  
- ![](@jurassic-saga:textures/item/natural/entity/animal/v1/extinct/troodon/troodon_fossil_remains) [**Troodon Remains**](@jurassic-saga:fossil)
- ![](@jurassic-saga:textures/item/natural/entity/animal/v1/extinct/troodon/troodon_raw) [**Troodon Raw Meat**](@jurassic-saga:meats)

## <img width={64} src="https://i.imgur.com/DTOgOBL.png" alt="Mr.DNA"/> Mr. DNA's Fun Trivia
- Troodon is theorized to have been one of the smartest dinosaurs, due to the size of its braincase!
- Troodon lived in the Judith River formation, alongside other dinosaurs like Daspletosaurus, Lokiceratops and Corythosaurus!
- Troodontids such as Troodon had a sickle claw, just like Dromaeosaurids such as Velociraptor!

- The achievement "They're Everywhere!" references the line shouted by Billy Yoder during the chapter "Separated!" as the Troodon attacks the group
- Troodon has the most aggressive venom out of the non-hybrid animals in the mod
