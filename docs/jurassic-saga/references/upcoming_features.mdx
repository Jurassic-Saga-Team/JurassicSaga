## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: When will these features be released?**  
A: Release dates are not guaranteed. Features will be added when they are ready and thoroughly tested.

**Q: Can I access these features early?**  
A: No, upcoming features are not available in any current version of the mod.

**Q: Will all of these features definitely be added?**  
A: While the development team intends to add these features, plans may change based on technical limitations or design decisions.

**Q: How can I stay updated on development progress?**  
A: Join the official [Discord server](https://discord.gg/3b8k78f) for the latest news and announcements.

## Trivia
- The upcoming features page gives players a glimpse into the future of Jurassic Saga
- Model previews are provided by the development team to build excitement for future updates
- All five revealed creatures are from the Triassic or Early Jurassic periods, expanding the mod's prehistoric timeline
- Postosuchus and Tanystropheus represent non-dinosaurian reptiles, adding diversity beyond traditional dinosaurs
- Some features may be added sooner than expected, while others may take longer to perfect
