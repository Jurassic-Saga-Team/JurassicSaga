# Boa imperator

*Boa imperator*, commonly known as the **Central American boa constrictor**, is a species of large, non-venomous constricting snake native to the **Americas**.
It is found from Mexico through Central America and into northwestern South America, where it inhabits a wide range of environments including rainforests, dry forests, and scrubland.

---

## Appearance
- Long, thick-bodied snake with a patterned coat of tan, brown, and cream saddles
- Reddish-brown markings become more vivid toward the tail
- Flat to the ground, making it easy to miss in grass and undergrowth
- In-game size: **2 blocks long** and just **0.1 blocks tall**

---

## Behavior
- **Hostile**: Boa Imperator will attack players that come near it
- Low profile makes it hard to spot until you are close
- Watch your step when travelling through its habitat

---

## Obtaining
Boa Imperator can be found as a **natural spawn** in the world.
It cannot be bred.

---

## Drops
Boa Imperator does not drop any items when defeated.
