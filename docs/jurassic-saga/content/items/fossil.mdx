# Fossils & Remains

**Fossils** and **Remains** are the two primary sources of biological material in *Jurassic Saga*.
Both are essential for crafting, progression, and extracting DNA used in creature resurrection.

- **Remains** come from *living creatures* (mob drops).  
- **Fossils** come from *ancient geological deposits* (underground fossil ores).  

Together, they form the backbone of the mod’s genetic system.

---

## Remains

Remains are biological leftovers obtained from specific creatures.  
Each species drops its own unique **Remains**, which can be processed for DNA or used in crafting.

### Obtaining Remains
- Dropped by defeating certain creatures.  
- Each species has a unique Remains item  
  - e.g., *Tyrannosaurus Remains*, *Velociraptor Remains*.

### Usage of Remains

#### Crafting
- Used in various recipes, including the [**Guidebook**](@jurassic-saga:guidebook).

#### Genetic Analyzer
- Insert Remains to extract a **random percentage of DNA** from that exact species.  
- DNA yield varies; collecting multiple samples increases accuracy and completion.

### Notes
- **Species‑specific** — Remains cannot be mixed or substituted.  
- A core component of the early‑game genetic progression loop.

---

## Fossils

Fossils are ancient biological traces preserved in stone and found as **Fossil Ores**.  
Each fossil ore corresponds to a specific **geological era** and **environment**:

- **Marine** — oceanic or coastal organisms  
- **Fluvial** — river, swamp, or floodplain organisms
