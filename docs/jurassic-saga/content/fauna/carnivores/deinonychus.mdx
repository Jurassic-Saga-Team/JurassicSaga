# Deinonychus antirrhopus

*Deinonychus*, meaning **"terrible claw,"** is a genus of dromaeosaurid theropod dinosaur from the **Early Cretaceous** of North America.  
It is one of the most significant dinosaurs in paleontological history — its discovery played a major role in establishing the connection between dinosaurs and modern birds.

---

## Appearance
- Medium-sized, lightly built dromaeosaurid with strong hind limbs
- Prominent sickle claw on the second toe, typical of raptors
- Likely feathered, though exact coverage is unknown
- **Male**: Rich dark-brown base coat with bold golden-yellow spots and blotches across the back, sides, and tail, reminiscent of a leopard's pattern; cream-white underbelly
- **Female**: Uniformly dark brown across the entire body with no spotting for better camouflage; cream-white underbelly
- In-game size: **4 blocks long** and **2 blocks tall**

---

## Behavior
- **Hostile**: Will only attack players and mobs when approached from behind — approaching head-on will not provoke an attack
- Fast and agile, making it a dangerous encounter if it gets behind you

---

## Obtaining
Deinonychus does not spawn naturally. To obtain one, you must:
1. Find ![](@jurassic-saga:textures/item/natural/entity/animal/v5/extinct/deinonychus/deinonychus_fossil_remains) [**Deinonychus Remains**](@jurassic-saga:fossil) or fossils
2. Use the fossil revival process to create a Deinonychus
3. Hatch from an **Alligator Egg** type egg

---
