# Dilophosaurus wetherilli

Dilophosaurus was a medium-sized theropod from the **Early Jurassic period**, discovered in what is now the southwestern United States.  
It is most recognizable for the pair of distinctive crests on its head, which were likely used for display or species recognition.  
Evidence suggests Dilophosaurus may have been a scavenger or opportunistic predator. Contrary to popular depictions, there is **no evidence** of venom or a neck frill.

---

## Appearance
- Slender body with long legs adapted for running
- Pair of thin, arched crests on the skull
- Sharp teeth and claws suited for opportunistic predation
- In-game size: up to **4 blocks in length** and **2 blocks in height**

---

## Behavior
- **Hostile**: Attacks players and other mobs on sight
- May hunt smaller creatures or scavenge remains
- Displays aggressive territorial behavior

---

## Obtaining
Dilophosaurus does not spawn naturally. To obtain one, you must:
1. Find ![](@jurassic-saga:textures/item/natural/entity/animal/v1/extinct/dilophosaurus/dilophosaurus_fossil_remains) [**Dilophosaurus Remains**](@jurassic-saga:fossil) or fossils
2. Use the fossil revival process to create a Dilophosaurus
3. Hatch from an **Alligator Egg** type egg

---

## Diet
- **Carnivore**: feeds on <img src="https://i.imgur.com/4K6xsJA.gif" width={32} height={32} style={{imageRendering: "pixelated"}}/> meat sources
- Opportunistic predator, may scavenge when hunting is not possible

---

## Drops
When defeated, Dilophosaurus may drop:  
- ![](@jurassic-saga:textures/item/natural/entity/animal/v1/extinct/dilophosaurus/dilophosaurus_fossil_remains) [**Dilophosaurus Remains**](@jurassic-saga:fossil)
- ![](@jurassic-saga:textures/item/natural/entity/animal/v1/extinct/dilophosaurus/dilophosaurus_raw) [**Dilophosaurus Raw Meat**](@jurassic-saga:meats)

## <img width={64} src="https://i.imgur.com/DTOgOBL.png" alt="Mr.DNA"/> Mr. DNA's Fun Trivia
- Dilophosaurus was one of first dinosaurs to become an apex predator!
- Dilophosaurus preyed on other dinosaurs, such as Sarahsaurus and Scutosaurus!
- Dilophosaurus lived in the Kayenta Formation around 195 to 183 million years ago, and it's ambients frequently alternated between wet seasons and arid conditions!
