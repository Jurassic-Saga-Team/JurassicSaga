# Gallimimus bullatus

Gallimimus was a fast, ostrich‑like dinosaur from the **Late Cretaceous period**, discovered in Mongolia.  
It had long legs adapted for speed and a toothless beak, suggesting a varied diet.  
Fossil evidence indicates Gallimimus may have moved in large groups, potentially for protection or migration.

---

## Appearance
- Slender, lightweight body built for running.  
- Long legs and tail providing balance and speed.  
- Small head with a toothless beak.  
- In‑game size: up to **7 blocks in length** and **4 blocks in height**.  

---

## Behavior
- **Neutral**: Gallimimus does not attack players unless provoked.  
- Moves quickly across terrain, often seen in herds.  
- Exhibits flocking behavior, reflecting its name and fossil evidence.  

---

## Diet
- **Herbivore** — feeds primarily on [Leaves](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Leaves).  
- May graze on low vegetation, mimicking its real‑world browsing habits.  

---

## Health
- **Adult**: 37 health (18.5 hearts)  

---
