# Tapejara wellnhoferi

*Tapejara* is a genus of pterosaur from the **Early Cretaceous** period, with fossils discovered in the **Santana Formation** of what is now **Ceará, Brazil**.
It is best known for its large, elaborate cranial crest, which differed significantly between males and females and is thought to have played a role in display and species recognition.
Despite its small size, Tapejara was a capable flier, likely hunting fish over coastal and inland waters.

---

## Appearance
- Small pterosaur with broad wings, a short neck, and a distinctive large cranial crest
- Displays strong sexual dimorphism in both colouration and crest appearance:
  - **Males** have a sandy tan-beige body with vivid deep red wings bearing blue accent patches on the crest and wing membrane, an orange-red beak and crest, and a warm yellow-orange eye
  - **Females** have a rich dark green body and wings with brighter lime-green highlights, a warm golden-brown face and beak, a large rounded green crest with lighter mottled patterning, and a cream-white chest and belly
- In-game size: **1 block long** and **1 block tall**

---

## Behavior
- **Passive**: Does not attack players or other creatures unprovoked
- Likely perches near water sources and takes to the air to hunt fish

---

## Obtaining
Tapejara does not spawn naturally. To obtain one, you must:
1. Enable [Experimental Features]($references/experimental)
2. Find ![](@jurassic-saga:textures/item/natural/entity/animal/[PATH]/tapejara_fossil_remains) [**Tapejara Remains**](@jurassic-saga:fossil) or fossils
3. Use the fossil revival process to create a Tapejara
4. Hatch from a **Chicken Egg** type egg

---

## Diet
- **Piscivore**: feeds on <img src="https://i.imgur.com/TG94al6.gif" width={32} height={32} style={{imageRendering: "pixelated"}}/> fish
- Likely hunted by swooping low over the water to snatch fish with its beak
