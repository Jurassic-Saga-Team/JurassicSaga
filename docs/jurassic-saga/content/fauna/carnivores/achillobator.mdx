# Achillobator giganticus

*Achillobator*, meaning **"Achilles hero,"** is a genus of large dromaeosaurid theropod dinosaur from the **Late Cretaceous** of Mongolia.  
It was one of the largest known dromaeosaurids, equipped with powerful legs, a large sickle claw, and a robust build suited for active predation.

---

## Appearance
- Large, heavily built dromaeosaurid with strong hind limbs
- Prominent sickle claw on the second toe, typical of raptors
- Likely feathered, though exact coverage is unknown
- In-game size: **5 blocks long** and **2 blocks tall**

---

## Behavior 
- **Hostile (Requires 2)**: A single Achillobator will not attack; when **two or more** are present, they become aggressive and will attack players and mobs
- Fast, coordinated, and dangerous in groups
- Often found roaming open terrain or hunting in pairs

---

## Obtaining
Achillobator does not spawn naturally. To obtain one, you must:
1. Find ![](@jurassic-saga:textures/item/natural/entity/animal/v2/extinct/achillobator/achillobator_fossil_remains) [**Achillobator Remains**](@jurassic-saga:fossil) or fossils
2. Use the fossil revival process to create an Achillobator
3. Hatch from an **Alligator Egg** type egg

---

## Diet
- **Carnivore**: feeds on <img src="https://i.imgur.com/4K6xsJA.gif" width={32} height={32} style={{imageRendering: "pixelated"}}/> meat sources
- Opportunistic predator, may scavenge when hunting is not possible

---

## Drops 
When defeated, Achillobator may drop: 
- ![](@jurassic-saga:textures/item/natural/entity/animal/v2/extinct/achillobator/achillobator_fossil_remains) [**Achillobator Remains**](@jurassic-saga:fossil)
- ![](@jurassic-saga:textures/item/natural/entity/animal/v2/extinct/achillobator/achillobator_raw) [**Achillobator Raw Meat**](@jurassic-saga:meats)

## <img width={64} src="https://i.imgur.com/DTOgOBL.png" alt="Mr.DNA"/> Mr. DNA's Fun Trivia
- Achillobator was one of the largest dromaeosaurs to ever exist, only being smaller than Dakotaraptor and Utahraptor!
- Achillobator lived in the Late Cretaceous of what is now Mongolia, alongside other animals like Segnosaurus and Talarurus!
- Achillobator's name means Achilles Hero, referencing the huge Achilles tendon it needed to use it's sickle claw!
- The achievement "Population: 37" references the scene in the first novel's Control V chapter where the raptor population is properly recounted by the park's system, the total population present on the island jumping from 8 raptors to 37.
- The Achillobator in Jurassic Saga is based on the novel iteration of Jurassic Park's Velociraptor<sup>[[9]](https://jurassicpark.fandom.com/wiki/Velociraptor/Novels)</sup>
