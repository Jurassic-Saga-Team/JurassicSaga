# Brachiosaurus altithorax

| **Size** | 22 Blocks Long, 19 Blocks Tall |
| **Drops** | Brachiosaurus Raw Meat, Brachiosaurus Remains |
| **Breedable** | Not yet implemented |
| **Obtained** | Fossil Revival |
| **Egg Type** | Ostrich Egg |

| **Achievement** | Reach For The Treetops |


*Brachiosaurus* was one of the largest sauropods of the **Late Jurassic period**, instantly recognizable by its towering neck and upright stance.
Unlike most sauropods, its forelimbs were longer than its hindlimbs, giving it a distinctive posture and allowing it to browse vegetation far above the reach of other dinosaurs.
It likely moved in herds and required enormous amounts of plant material each day to sustain its size.

---

## Appearance
- Long neck held upright, enabling feeding from tall trees
- Forelimbs longer than hindlimbs, creating a sloping back
- Massive body with a small head compared to its size
- In-game size: **22 blocks long** and **19 blocks tall**, making it one of the tallest creatures in Jurassic Saga

---

## Behavior
- **Neutral**: Will not attack unless provoked
- Moves slowly in groups, grazing on leaves and plant matter
- Occasionally rests or sleeps, reflecting herd life

---

## Obtaining
Brachiosaurus does not spawn naturally. To obtain one, you must:
1. Find ![](@jurassic-saga:textures/item/natural/entity/animal/v1/extinct/brachiosaurus/brachiosaurus_fossil_remains) [**Brachiosaurus Remains**]($content/items/fossil) or fossils
2. Use the fossil revival process to create a Brachiosaurus
3. Hatch from an **Ostrich Egg** type egg

---

## Diet
- **Herbivore**: feeds primarily on <img src="https://i.imgur.com/INKBvsq.gif" width={32} height={32} style={{imageRendering: "pixelated"}}/> [**Leaves**](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Leaves)
- Prefers taller vegetation, matching its elevated posture

---

## Drops
When defeated, Brachiosaurus may drop:
- ![](@jurassic-saga:textures/item/natural/entity/animal/v1/extinct/brachiosaurus/brachiosaurus_fossil_remains) [**Brachiosaurus Remains**]($content/items/fossil)
- ![](@jurassic-saga:textures/item/natural/entity/animal/v1/extinct/brachiosaurus/brachiosaurus_raw) [**Brachiosaurus Raw Meat**]($content/items/meats)
