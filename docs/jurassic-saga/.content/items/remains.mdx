# Remains

Remains are special items obtained by killing a specific creature.  
They represent the biological leftovers of that species and serve as a key resource in crafting and genetic research.

---

## Obtaining
- Dropped when defeating certain creatures.  
- Each species drops its own unique **Remains** item.  

---

## Usage
- **Crafting**:  
  - Used in recipes such as the [**Guidebook**](@jurassic-saga:guidebook).  
- **Genetic Analyzer**:  
  - Can be placed inside the analyzer to extract a random percentage of DNA from the exact species the remains belong to.  
  - DNA percentages vary, making multiple samples useful for higher accuracy.
