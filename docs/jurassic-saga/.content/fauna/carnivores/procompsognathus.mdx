# Procompsognathus triassicus

Procompsognathus was a small, lightly built dinosaur from the **Late Triassic period** of Europe, with fossils discovered in Germany.  
It was a fast runner and likely insectivorous or carnivorous, feeding on insects and small vertebrates.  
Its small size and speed made it effective at darting through underbrush, and it may have lived in groups for protection.

---

## Appearance
- Small, slender theropod with a lightweight frame
- Long legs adapted for quick bursts of speed
- Narrow snout with small, sharp teeth suited for catching insects and tiny prey
- In-game size: **2 blocks long** and **1 block tall**

---

## Behavior
- **Hostile (Requires 3)**:  
    - A single Procompsognathus will not attack the player
    - When **three or more** are present, they become aggressive and swarm their target
- Uses quick darting movements to harass prey and overwhelm through numbers

---

## Obtaining
Procompsognathus does not spawn naturally. To obtain one, you must:
1. Find ![](@jurassic-saga:textures/item/natural/entity/animal/v1/extinct/procompsognathus/procompsognathus_fossil_remains) [**Procompsognathus Remains**](@jurassic-saga:fossil) or fossils
2. Use the fossil revival process to create a Procompsognathus
3. Hatch from a **Chicken Egg** type egg

---

## Diet
- **Carnivore**: feeds on <img src="https://i.imgur.com/4K6xsJA.gif" width={32} height={32} style={{imageRendering: "pixelated"}}/> meat sources
- Hunts small creatures and scavenges scraps left by larger predators

## Trivia
- The achievement "Caution: Venom" references the venomous bite attributed to "Compys" in Michael Crichton's *Jurassic Park* novels, though there is no scientific evidence for venom in real Procompsognathus
- In the *Jurassic Park* franchise, Procompsognathus is often conflated with Compsognathus, with both being referred to as "Compys"
