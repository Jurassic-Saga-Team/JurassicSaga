*Styracosaurus* is an extinct genus of herbivorous ceratopsian dinosaur from the **Late Cretaceous**.  
It is known for its dramatic frill adorned with four to six long spikes, as well as a prominent nasal horn and smaller cheek horns.

---

## Appearance
- Large ceratopsian with a distinctive spiked frill
- Prominent nasal horn and smaller jugal (cheek) horns
- Robust, muscular body built for defense and stability
- In-game size: **5 blocks long** and **3 blocks tall**

---

## Behavior
- **Neutral**: Will not attack unless provoked
- Retaliates when attacked
- Often found in small defensive groups
- Uses its horns and frill to intimidate predators and protect herd members

---

## Obtaining
Styracosaurus does not spawn naturally. To obtain one, you must:
1. Find ![](@jurassic-saga:textures/item/natural/entity/animal/v2/extinct/styracosaurus/styracosaurus_fossil_remains) [**Styracosaurus Remains**]($content/items/fossil) or fossils
2. Use the fossil revival process to create a Styracosaurus
3. Hatch from an **Ostrich Egg** type egg

---

## Diet
- **Herbivore**: feeds primarily on <img src="https://i.imgur.com/INKBvsq.gif" width={32} height={32} style={{imageRendering: "pixelated"}}/> [**Leaves**](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Leaves)
- Grazes on shrubs and low vegetation

---

## Drops
When defeated, Styracosaurus may drop:
- ![](@jurassic-saga:textures/item/natural/entity/animal/v2/extinct/styracosaurus/styracosaurus_fossil_remains) [**Styracosaurus Remains**]($content/items/fossil)
- ![](@jurassic-saga:textures/item/natural/entity/animal/v2/extinct/styracosaurus/styracosaurus_raw) [**Styracosaurus Raw Meat**]($content/items/meats)

## <img width={64} src="https://i.imgur.com/DTOgOBL.png" alt="Mr.DNA"/> Mr. DNA's Fun Trivia
- Styracosaurus had one of the most unique skulls among ceratopsians, with various horns and a single long horn at the tip of it's head, making it an intimidating sight for even the big predators!
- Styracosaurus lived alongside animals like Chasmosaurus and Corythosaurus, in what is now North America!
- Styracosaurus' beak was built for slicing up plants, such as prehistoric cycads, ferns and palms!
