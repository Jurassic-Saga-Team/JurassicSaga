# Fossils & Remains

**Fossils** and **Remains** are the two primary sources of biological material in Jurassic Saga.  
Both are essential for crafting, progression, and extracting DNA for creature resurrection.

- **Remains** come from *living creatures* (mob drops).  
- **Fossils** come from *ancient geological deposits* (ores found underground).  

Together, they form the backbone of the mod’s genetic system.

---

# Remains

Remains are biological leftovers obtained by killing a specific creature.  
Each species drops its own unique **Remains**, which can be processed for DNA or used in crafting.

---

## Obtaining Remains
- Dropped when defeating certain creatures.  
- Each species has its own unique Remains item (e.g., *Tyrannosaurus Remains*, *Velociraptor Remains*).  

---

## Usage of Remains
- **Crafting**  
  - Used in recipes such as the [**Guidebook**](@jurassic-saga:guidebook).  

- **Genetic Analyzer**  
  - Insert Remains to extract a random percentage of DNA from that exact species.  
  - DNA yield varies, so multiple samples increase accuracy.  

---

## Notes on Remains
- Species‑specific — cannot be mixed or substituted.  
- A core component of the genetic progression system.  

---

# Fossils

Fossils are ancient remains embedded in stone, found as **Fossil Ores** throughout the world.  
Each fossil ore corresponds to a specific **geological era** and **environment** (marine or fluvial).

# Trivia
- Fossils and Remains both feed into the same genetic system, but come from different sources.  
- Fossils represent ancient life; Remains represent modern creatures.  
- Some fossil drops (like amber or deposits) can contain rare DNA fragments.
