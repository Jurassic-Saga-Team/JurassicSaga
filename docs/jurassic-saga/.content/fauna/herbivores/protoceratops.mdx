# Protoceratops hellenikorhinus

*Protoceratops* is a genus of small protoceratopsid dinosaurs that lived in Asia during the **Late Cretaceous**, around 75–71 million years ago.  
The genus includes two species: *P. andrewsi* and the larger *P. hellenikorhinus*.  
It is often depicted locked in combat with *Velociraptor*, based on a famous fossil find.

---

## Appearance
- Small ceratopsian with a short frill and beaked mouth.  
- Robust body and strong limbs for digging or foraging.  
- Lacked horns but had a characteristic frill shape unique to the genus.  
- Adults reach up to **5 blocks in length** and **2 blocks in height** in‑game.  

---

## Behavior
- **Neutral**:  
  - Will retaliate when attacked.  
  - Often found in small groups, reflecting herd‑like behavior.  
  - Uses its beak and body weight to defend itself when cornered.  

---

## Diet
- **Herbivore**  
- Feeds primarily on:  
  - ![](@jurassic-saga:gifs/leaves.gif) [**Leaves**](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Leaves)  
- In‑game, it grazes on shrubs and low vegetation.
