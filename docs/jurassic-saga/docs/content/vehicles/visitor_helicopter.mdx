# Visitor Helicopter

| | |
|---|---|

| **Passengers** | 4-7 Seats |

| **Speed** | Fast |
| **Fuel** | Not Required |

| **Craftable** | Not yet implemented |
| **Obtained** | Creative Mode Only |


The Visitor Helicopter is an aerial transport vehicle designed to carry multiple passengers safely across the island.  
Based on the iconic helicopter from *Jurassic Park* (1993) and *Jurassic Park: Operation Genesis*, it serves as a quick way to scout terrain, transport groups, or escape dangerous situations from above.

---

## Appearance
- Design inspired by the JPOG and JP (1993) Visitor Helicopter
- White and blue livery with a distinctive yellow/black/blue horizontal stripe running along the fuselage
- Registration number "N293G" displayed on the tail and sides
- Large windshield providing excellent visibility for pilot and passengers
- Rotating rotor blades during flight
- Retractable landing gear
- Spacious interior with multiple passenger seats

---

## Functionality
- **Type**: Air vehicle
- **Capacity**: 4-7 passengers (configuration varies)
- **Speed**: Fast aerial movement
- **Fuel**: Not required
- **Durability**: 100 hearts (200 health points)
- **Special Features**: Vertical takeoff and landing, hover capability, retractable landing gear, high vantage point for spotting creatures

---

## Controls
- **Ascend**: Space
- **Forward/Backward**: W/S
- **Strafe Left/Right**: A/D
- **Toggle Landing Gear**: Z
- **Enter Seat**: Right-click on helicopter
- **Exit Vehicle**: Shift

---

## Obtaining

### Crafting Recipe
Not yet craftable. Recipe will be added in a future update.

### Alternative Methods
- Currently available only through **Creative Mode**
- Future updates may include world spawns or crafting recipes

---

## Usage Tips
- Use the helicopter to quickly scout large areas and locate dinosaurs from a safe altitude
- Perfect for transporting groups of players across difficult terrain
- Toggle landing gear with Z before landing to ensure a smooth touchdown
- The helicopter's durability makes it vulnerable to large predatorsavoid flying too low near hostile creatures
- Take advantage of the hover capability to observe herds or assess situations before landing
- Coordinate with passengers when entering and exiting to avoid accidents

## Trivia
- The Visitor Helicopter is directly inspired by the Bell 212 helicopter that appears in *Jurassic Park* (1993), which brought visitors to Isla Nublar
- The registration number "N293G" is a reference to the original helicopter from the film
- In *Jurassic Park: Operation Genesis*, the helicopter was a key transportation method for viewing dinosaurs and managing the park
- The blue and white color scheme with the distinctive stripe matches the InGen branding seen throughout the Jurassic Park franchise
