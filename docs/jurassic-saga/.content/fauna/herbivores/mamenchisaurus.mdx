# Mamenchisaurus sinocanadorum

*Mamenchisaurus* is an extinct genus of sauropod dinosaurs known for their extraordinarily long necks, which made up nearly half their total body length.
They lived during the Late Jurassic in what is now China and are among the longest‑necked dinosaurs ever discovered.

---

## Appearance
- Extremely long neck, accounting for almost half of its total body length.
- Massive, pillar‑like legs supporting a long, heavy torso.
- Thick, muscular tail used for balance and defense.
- Small head with peg‑like teeth adapted for stripping vegetation.
- Adults reach up to **47 blocks in length** and **17 blocks in height** in‑game.  

---

## Behavior
- **Neutral**: 
    - Will not attack unless provoked.
    - When threatened, may swing its tail defensively.
- Moves slowly and peacefully through forests and open plains.
- Often travels in small groups, grazing together.
- Occasionally rears its neck upward to feed from tall trees.

---

## Diet
- **Herbivore**: feeds primarily on ![](@jurassic-saga:gifs/leaves.gif) [**Leaves**](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Leaves).  
- May also consume tall vegetation and tree canopies due to its extreme neck height.
