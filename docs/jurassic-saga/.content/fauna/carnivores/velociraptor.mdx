# Velociraptor mongoliensis

Velociraptor was a small, agile predator from the **Late Cretaceous period**, with fossils discovered in Mongolia and China.  
It measured around 2 meters in length and was covered in feathers, though it could not fly.  
Velociraptor is best known for its sickle‑shaped claw on each foot, which it likely used to pin down prey.  
It may have hunted small animals or scavenged, and evidence suggests it could have worked in packs to take down larger prey.

---

## Appearance
- Slender, lightweight body built for speed and agility.  
- Covered in feathers, giving it a bird‑like appearance.  
- Long, stiff tail for balance during running and leaping.  
- Distinctive sickle‑shaped claw on each foot.  
- In‑game size: up to **5 blocks in length** and **2 blocks in height**.  

---

## Behavior
- **Hostile**: Velociraptor will attack players and mobs on sight.  
- May coordinate in small groups, reflecting its reputation as a pack hunter.  
- Uses speed and agility to dart in and out of combat, making it a dangerous foe.  

---

## Diet
- **Carnivore**: feeds on meat sources.  
- Likely preyed on small animals, but may scavenge remains or attack larger creatures in groups.  

---

## Health
- **Adult**: 50 health (x25 hearts)
