# Spinosaurus aegyptiacus

*Spinosaurus* is a genus of large spinosaurid theropod dinosaur that lived in what is now North Africa during the **Cenomanian stage of the Late Cretaceous**.
It is one of the largest known carnivorous dinosaurs and is easily recognized by its elongated snout and distinctive sail.

---

## Appearance
- Long, crocodile-like snout filled with conical teeth adapted for catching fish
- Large neural spines forming a tall sail along its back
- Strong forelimbs with large claws used for grasping prey
- Semi-aquatic body proportions with a deep tail and robust hind limbs
- In-game size: **18 blocks long** and **8 blocks tall**, making it one of the largest creatures in Jurassic Saga

---

## Behavior
- **Hostile**: Will attack players and most mobs on sight
- Shows increased aggression near water sources
- Prefers riverbanks, swamps, and coastal areas
- Hunts both land and aquatic prey, often wading into water to pursue targets
- May roar or posture when encountering other large predators

---

## Obtaining
Spinosaurus does not spawn naturally. To obtain one, you must:
1. Enable [Experimental Features]($references/experimental)
2. Find ![](@jurassic-saga:textures/item/natural/entity/animal/v4/extinct/spinosaurus/spinosaurus_fossil_remains) [**Spinosaurus Remains**](@jurassic-saga:fossil) or fossils
3. Use the fossil revival process to create a Spinosaurus
4. Hatch from an **Ostrich Egg** type egg

---

## Diet
- **Piscivore**: feeds primarily on <img src="https://i.imgur.com/qICGNC1.gif" width={32} height={32} style={{imageRendering: "pixelated"}}/> fish sources
- Particularly effective at hunting aquatic creatures due to its long snout and strong bite

## Trivia
- The achievement "I don't remember that on the list" is a reference to Dr. Alan Grant's line from *Jurassic Park III* (2001) when he first sees the Spinosaurus
- Spinosaurus is believed to be the largest carnivorous dinosaur ever discovered, potentially surpassing even Tyrannosaurus rex in size
- Recent paleontological discoveries suggest Spinosaurus was highly adapted for an aquatic lifestyle, with dense bones and a paddle-like tail
