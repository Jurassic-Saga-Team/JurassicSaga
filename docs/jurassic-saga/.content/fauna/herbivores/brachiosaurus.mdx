# Brachiosaurus altithorax

Brachiosaurus was one of the largest sauropods of the **Late Jurassic period**, instantly recognizable by its towering neck and upright stance.  
Unlike most sauropods, its forelimbs were longer than its hindlimbs, giving it a distinctive posture and allowing it to browse vegetation far above the reach of other dinosaurs.  
It likely moved in herds and required enormous amounts of plant material each day to sustain its size.

---

## Appearance
- Long neck held upright, enabling feeding from tall trees.  
- Forelimbs longer than hindlimbs, creating a sloping back.  
- Massive body with a small head compared to its size.  
- Adults reach up to **22 blocks in length** and **19 blocks in height** in‑game.  

---

## Behavior
- **Neutral**: Brachiosaurus does not attack players unless provoked.  
- Moves slowly in groups, grazing on leaves and plant matter.  
- Occasionally rests or sleeps, reflecting herd life.  

---

## Diet
- **Herbivore**: feeds primarily on <img src="https://i.imgur.com/INKBvsq.gif" width={32} height={32} style={{imageRendering: "pixelated"}}/> [**Leaves**](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Leaves).  
- Prefers taller vegetation, matching its elevated posture.
