# Baryonyx walkeri

*Baryonyx* is a genus of theropod dinosaur that lived during the **Barremian stage of the Early Cretaceous**, around 130–125 million years ago. 
Its first skeleton was discovered in 1983 in Surrey, England, making it one of the most complete spinosaurid fossils ever found. 

---

## Appearance
- Long, crocodile-like snout filled with conical teeth
- Large claws on the forelimbs, including the iconic **30 cm "heavy claw"**
- Semi-aquatic build with a narrow skull and strong arms
- In-game size: **10 blocks long**, **3 blocks tall**

---

## Behavior
- **Hostile**: Will attack players and most mobs on sight
- Prefers to stay near rivers, lakes, and wetlands
- Uses its claws and bite in combat, making it dangerous at close range

---

## Obtaining
Baryonyx does not spawn naturally. To obtain one, you must:
1. Find ![](@jurassic-saga:textures/item/natural/entity/animal/v2/extinct/baryonyx/baryonyx_fossil_remains) [**Baryonyx Remains**](@jurassic-saga:fossil) or fossils
2. Use the fossil revival process to create a Baryonyx
3. Hatch from an **Alligator Egg** type egg

---

## Diet
- **Carnivore**: feeds on <img src="https://i.imgur.com/4K6xsJA.gif" width={32} height={32} style={{imageRendering: "pixelated"}}/> meat sources
- Specializes in fish but will eat meat of any kind

---

## Drops
When defeated, Baryonyx may drop:
- ![](@jurassic-saga:textures/item/natural/entity/animal/v2/extinct/baryonyx/baryonyx_fossil_remains) [**Baryonyx Remains**](@jurassic-saga:fossil)
- ![](@jurassic-saga:textures/item/natural/entity/animal/v2/extinct/baryonyx/baryonyx_raw) [**Baryonyx Raw Meat**](@jurassic-saga:meats)

## <img width={64} src="https://i.imgur.com/DTOgOBL.png" alt="Mr.DNA"/> Mr. DNA's Fun Trivia
- The Legacy skin comes from the old JurassiCraft Male Baryonyx Skin<sup>[[3]](https://pin.it/32E4um9Ms)</sup> (Shoutout to @rodmega10 on Discord for pointing it out!)
- Baryonyx's name "Heavy Claw", references it's big claw, characteristic of Baryonychines, which was primarily used for catching prey, such as Lepidotes!
- Baryonyx lived alongside two of it's spinosaurid relatives, those being Ceratosuchops and Riparovenator!
- Baryonyx was discovered by a plumber, William J. Walker, who was also an amateur fossil hunter at the time!
