## Fossil Ore Locations

Fossil Ores are found underground at specific Y-levels depending on their geological era.
Mine them to obtain Deposits, Amber, and other materials that can be analyzed for DNA.

> Drop percentages are based on testing samples of 64 blocks per ore type. Results may vary.
