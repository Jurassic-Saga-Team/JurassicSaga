# Alvarezsaurus calvoi

*Alvarezsaurus*, named in honor of the Argentine historian **Gregorio Álvarez**, is a genus of small, lightly built alvarezsaurid theropod dinosaur from the **Late Cretaceous** of Argentina.
Fast and slender, it was equipped with long legs and short, specialized forelimbs, making it a nimble hunter of small prey.

---

## Appearance
- Small, slender theropod with long, powerful hind limbs built for speed
- Very short arms ending in a prominent claw, suited for tearing into insect nests
- Long, narrow snout filled with tiny teeth
- Covered in dark green and grey camouflage-like mottling, with darker, near-black legs
- Shaggy feather tufts on the head, forearms and belly, giving it a fluffy outline
- In-game size: **2 blocks long** and **1 block tall**

---

## Behavior
- **Neutral**: Alvarezsaurus will not attack unprovoked, but will defend itself if attacked
- Quick and agile, it prefers to flee or keep its distance from larger threats
- Often found darting around open terrain in search of small prey

---

## Obtaining
Alvarezsaurus does not spawn naturally. To obtain one, you must:
1. Find ![](@jurassic-saga:inventory/alvarezsaurus_fossil_remains) [**Alvarezsaurus Remains**]($content/items/fossil) or fossils
2. Use the fossil revival process to create an Alvarezsaurus
3. Grow it in a **Gestation Chamber**

---

## Diet
- **Carnivore**: feeds on <img src="https://i.imgur.com/4K6xsJA.gif" width={32} height={32} style={{imageRendering: "pixelated"}}/> meat sources
- Opportunistic hunter of small prey, may scavenge when hunting is not possible

## <img width={64} src="https://i.imgur.com/DTOgOBL.png" alt="Mr.DNA"/> Mr. DNA's Fun Trivia
- Alvarezsaurus and other Alvarezsaurids have a peculiar single enlarged finger, a feature unique to these dinosaurs, which they may use to crack open eggs and dig up termite mounds!
- Alvarezsaurus lived around 86 to 83 million years ago, in what is now Argentina, it was named after the argentine historian Don Gregorio Alvarez!
- Alvarezsaurus is considered a basal member of Alvarezsauridae, meaning it branched off early from it's group's evolutionary tree and as such retains some ancestral traits, compared to some other members of it's family, such as Mononykus and Parvicursor!
