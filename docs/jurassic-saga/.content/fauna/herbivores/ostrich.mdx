# Ostrich

The ostrich is the largest living bird, native to the savannas and deserts of **Africa**.  
It is flightless but highly adapted for running, with long legs capable of reaching speeds up to 70 km/h.  
Ostriches are known for their distinctive long necks, powerful legs, and large eggs — the biggest of any living bird.

---

## Appearance
- Tall, flightless bird with long neck and legs.  
- Small head with large eyes for keen vision.  
- Plumage varies, with males often darker and females lighter brown.  
- In‑game size: up to **3 blocks in length** and **3 blocks in height**.  

---

## Behavior
- **Neutral**: Ostriches do not attack players unless provoked.  
- Moves quickly across terrain, often in small groups.  
- May lay eggs, reflecting their real‑world nesting behavior.
