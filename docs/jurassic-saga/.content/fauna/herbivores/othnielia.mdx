# Othnielosaurus consors

*Othnielia* is a genus of hypsilophodontid dinosaur featured in **Jurassic Park (1992)**.  
In Jurassic Saga, it represents a small, agile herbivore often found darting between vegetation in lightly wooded areas.

---

## Appearance
- Small, lightweight body built for speed and agility.  
- Narrow head with a beaked mouth suited for cropping vegetation.  
- Long tail used for balance while running.  
- Adults reach up to **3 blocks in length** and **1 block in height** in‑game.  

---

## Behavior
- **Neutral**:  
  - Will retaliate when attacked.  
  - Often flees from larger predators or players when alone.  
  - Moves in small groups, staying alert and skittish.  

---

## Diet
- **Herbivore**  
- Feeds primarily on:  
  - ![](@jurassic-saga:gifs/leaves.gif) [**Leaves**](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Leaves)  
- In‑game, it grazes on low vegetation and shrubs.  

---

## Health
- **Adult**: 15 health (x7.5 hearts)
