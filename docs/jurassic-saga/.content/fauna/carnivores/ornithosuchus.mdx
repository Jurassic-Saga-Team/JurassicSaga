# Ornithosuchus woodwardi

Ornithosuchus, meaning "Bird Crocodile," is an extinct genus of pseudosuchian from the **Late Triassic** of Scotland.  
It was originally thought to be the ancestor of the carnosaurian dinosaurs (such as Allosaurus), before it was revealed to be actually closer to crocodilians than dinosaurs.

---

## Appearance
- Medium-sized pseudosuchian with a crocodile-like build
- Powerful jaws filled with sharp, serrated teeth
- Strong limbs capable of both quadrupedal and bipedal movement
- Long tail for balance
- In-game size: **4 blocks long** and **1 block tall**

---

## Behavior 
- **Hostile**: Will attack players and most mobs on sight
- Fast, coordinated, and dangerous in groups
- Often found roaming open terrain or hunting in pairs

---

## Obtaining
Ornithosuchus does not spawn naturally. To obtain one, you must:
1. Find ![](@jurassic-saga:textures/item/natural/entity/animal/v3/extinct/ornithosuchus/ornithosuchus_fossil_remains) [**Ornithosuchus Remains**](@jurassic-saga:fossil) or fossils
2. Use the fossil revival process to create an Ornithosuchus
3. Hatch from an **Alligator Egg** type egg

---

## Diet
- **Carnivore**: feeds on <img src="https://i.imgur.com/4K6xsJA.gif" width={32} height={32} style={{imageRendering: "pixelated"}}/> meat sources
- Opportunistic predator, may scavenge when hunting is not possible

---

## Drops 
When defeated, Ornithosuchus may drop: 
- ![](@jurassic-saga:textures/item/natural/entity/animal/v3/extinct/ornithosuchus/ornithosuchus_fossil_remains) [**Ornithosuchus Remains**](@jurassic-saga:fossil)

## Trivia
- The achievement "Code name: 'Gnasher'" may reference Ornithosuchus's powerful bite and predatory nature
- Despite being called "Bird Crocodile," Ornithosuchus is neither a bird nor a crocodile—it's a pseudosuchian, more closely related to modern crocodilians than to dinosaurs
- Ornithosuchus could walk both on four legs and on two legs, making it one of the more versatile predators of the Late Triassic
