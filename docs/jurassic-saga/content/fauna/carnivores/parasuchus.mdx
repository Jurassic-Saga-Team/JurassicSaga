## <img width={64} src="https://i.imgur.com/DTOgOBL.png" alt="Mr.DNA"/> Mr. DNA's Fun Trivia
- Parasuchus lived in the Late Triassic of what is now India, belonging to an order of archosauriformes called Phytosauria, which despite looking like crocodiles, were a completely separate lineage!
- Parasuchus was initially deemed a chimera, meaning it would've been actually multiple animals that were mistaken as only one, until new fossils of it were described in the late 1970s!
- Parasuchus' name means "Near Crocodile", in reference to it looking like a crocodile despite belonging to a separate lineage of animals!
