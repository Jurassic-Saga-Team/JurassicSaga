Cearadactylus was a pterosaur from the **Early Cretaceous period**, discovered in Brazil.  
It had a long, toothed beak adapted for catching fish, making it a specialized piscivore.  
With a wingspan of around **4–5 meters**, it was a capable flyer that likely soared over coastal and river environments.

---

## Appearance
- Long, narrow skull with sharp teeth suited for gripping fish
- Large wings with membranous structure, typical of pterosaurs
- Lightweight body adapted for flight
- In-game size: **3 blocks long** and **2 blocks tall**

---

## Behavior
- **Passive**: Flees when attacked, taking to the air if possible
- Often seen gliding above water sources in search of fish
- Will perch on rocks or trees when resting
- Avoids predators and large carnivores

---

## Obtaining
Cearadactylus does not spawn naturally. To obtain one, you must:
1. Find ![](@jurassic-saga:textures/item/natural/entity/animal/v1/extinct/cearadactylus/cearadactylus_fossil_remains) [**Cearadactylus Remains**]($content/items/fossil) or fossils
2. Use the fossil revival process to create a Cearadactylus
3. Hatch from an **Alligator Egg** type egg

---

## Diet
- **Piscivore**: feeds primarily on <img src="https://i.imgur.com/qICGNC1.gif" width={32} height={32} style={{imageRendering: "pixelated"}}/> fish sources
- Swoops down to catch fish from rivers, lakes, and coastal areas

---

## Drops
When defeated, Cearadactylus may drop:
- ![](@jurassic-saga:textures/item/natural/entity/animal/v1/extinct/cearadactylus/cearadactylus_fossil_remains) [**Cearadactylus Remains**]($content/items/fossil)
- ![](@jurassic-saga:textures/item/natural/entity/animal/v1/extinct/cearadactylus/cearadactylus_raw) [**Cearadactylus Raw Meat**]($content/items/meats)

## <img width={64} src="https://i.imgur.com/DTOgOBL.png" alt="Mr.DNA"/> Mr. DNA's Fun Trivia
- Cearadactylus gets it's name from the Brazilian state Ceará!
- Cearadactylus lived alongside animals such as Irritator, Tapejara and Tropeognathus!
- Cearadactylus' interlocking teeth were perfect for catching fish, as it lived in the coasts of Brazil!

---

## Trivia
- The achievement "Dive-Bombing Menace" references Cearadactylus's hunting behavior of swooping down to catch fish from the water
- Cearadactylus means "Ceará finger," named after the Ceará State in Brazil where it was discovered
- Unlike many pterosaurs, Cearadactylus had teeth along the entire length of its jaws, making it highly specialized for catching slippery prey
