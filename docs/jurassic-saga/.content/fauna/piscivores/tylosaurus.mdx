# Tylosaurus proriger

Tylosaurus was a massive marine reptile from the **Late Cretaceous period**, belonging to the mosasaur family.  
It could reach over **14 meters in length**, making it one of the largest mosasaurs.  
As a top predator, it fed on fish, other marine reptiles, and even smaller mosasaurs, dominating the ancient seas of North America.

---

## Appearance
- Long, streamlined body adapted for swimming.  
- Powerful tail used for propulsion.  
- Large skull with conical teeth suited for gripping prey.  
- In‑game size: up to **18 blocks in length** and **2 blocks in height**.  

---

## Behavior
- **Hostile**: Tylosaurus will attack players and mobs on sight.  
- Reflects its role as a prehistoric apex predator.  
- Often encountered in deep ocean biomes, making it a dangerous threat to explorers.  

---

## Diet
- **Piscivore** — specialized in eating fish and other marine life.  
- In‑game food sources: **Meats, Fish, Insects**.  

---

## Health
- **Adult**: 70 health (x35 hearts)
