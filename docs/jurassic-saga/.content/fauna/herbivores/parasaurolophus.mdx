# Parasaurolophus cyrtocristatus

Parasaurolophus was a herbivorous hadrosaurid from the **Late Cretaceous period**, known for its distinctive long, backward‑curving crest.  
The crest may have served multiple functions, including visual display, sound resonance for communication, and possibly thermoregulation.  
Parasaurolophus likely lived in herds and grazed on vegetation, making it one of the most recognizable “duck‑billed” dinosaurs.

---

## Appearance
- Long, backward‑curving crest extending from the skull.  
- Broad, duck‑bill snout adapted for cropping plants.  
- Strong hind limbs with a long tail for balance.  
- In‑game size: up to **12 blocks in length** and **5 blocks in height**.  

---

## Behavior
- **Neutral**: Parasaurolophus does not attack players unless provoked.  
- Moves steadily in herds, grazing on vegetation.  
- May use its crest for sound resonance, reflecting its real‑world communication abilities.  

---

## Diet
- **Herbivore** — feeds primarily on [Leaves](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Leaves).  
- Browses on mid‑level vegetation, consistent with its duck‑bill anatomy.  

---

## Health
- **Adult**: 85 health (x42.5 hearts)
