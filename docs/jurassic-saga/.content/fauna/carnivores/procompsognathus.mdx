# Procompsognathus triassicus

Procompsognathus was a small, lightly built dinosaur from the **Late Triassic period** of Europe, with fossils discovered in Germany.  
It was a fast runner and likely insectivorous or carnivorous, feeding on insects and small vertebrates.  
Its small size and speed made it effective at darting through underbrush, and it may have lived in groups for protection.

---

## Appearance
- Slender body with long legs adapted for speed.  
- Small head with sharp teeth suited for catching insects and small prey.  
- Lightweight frame, allowing quick movement through vegetation.  
- In‑game size: up to **2 blocks in length** and **1 block in height**.  

---

## Behavior
- **Hostile**: Procompsognathus will attack players and mobs, but only when in sufficient numbers.  
- **Swarm Attack**: A single Procomp will not engage alone. They require a minimum group size to attack, which scales with the target’s health.  
- Example: A player with 10 hearts (20 HP) requires at least 3 Procomps before they will swarm.  
- The higher the target’s health pool, the more Procomps are needed to trigger aggression.  
- This mechanic makes them deceptively dangerous, individually weak, but capable of overwhelming stronger players when attacking in groups.  


---

## Diet
- **Carnivore**: feeds on meat sources.  
- Likely insectivorous, targeting small animals and invertebrates.  

---

## Health
- **Adult**: 9 health (x4.5 hearts)
