## Machines & Systems
- **Machines**: Core crafting and processing units used to create dinosaurs.  
- **Feeders**: Automated systems that provide food to dinosaurs, ensuring their health and proper care.  
- **Vehicle**: A transport option for traversing the world quickly and efficiently.  
- **Achievements**: Unlock milestones tied to systems, species, and progression within the mod.  

---

## Items & Dinosaurs
- **Items**: Tools, consumables, and crafting components.  
- **Plants & Trees**: New flora introduced by the mod, used for decoration, resources, and ecosystem building.  
- **Decorative & Building Blocks**: Structures and materials for customizing enclosures, laboratories, and player bases.  
- **Dinosaurs**: Stats, regions, diets, and behaviors.  
- **Drops & Remains**: Uses and collection methods.
