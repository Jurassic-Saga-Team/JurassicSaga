# Fossils

Fossils are ancient remains embedded in stone, representing different geological eras.  
They appear as **Fossil Ores**, each tied to a specific time period and environment (marine or fluvial).

---

## Example Drops

Drops vary depending on fossil type.  
Below are sample results from testing **64 blocks** of each ore:

- **Carboniferous Marine Fossil Ore**  
  - 28.8% Cobblestone  
  - 29.4% Bones  
  - 9.4% Carboniferous Marine Deposit  
  - 32.5% Flint  

- **Carboniferous Fluvial Fossil Ore**  
  - 18.5% Cobblestone  
  - 8.1% Carboniferous Plant Fossil  
  - 21.3% Flint  
  - 10.9% Carboniferous Megabug Part in Amber  
  - 14.7% Bones  
  - 26.5% Carboniferous Plants in Amber
