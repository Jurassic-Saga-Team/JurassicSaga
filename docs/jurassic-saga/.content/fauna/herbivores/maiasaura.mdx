# Maiasaura peeblesorum

*Maiasaura* is a hadrosaurid dinosaur famous for its reputation as the **“good mother lizard.”**  
Fossil evidence shows that Maiasaura cared for its young in nesting colonies, returning to the same nesting grounds year after year.  
Lightweight for its size and capable of running, Maiasaura was a social, herd‑dwelling herbivore of the Late Cretaceous.

---

## Appearance
- Medium‑sized hadrosaur with a long tail and sturdy hind limbs.  
- Rounded snout and beak adapted for cropping vegetation.  
- Smooth, streamlined body typical of hadrosaurids.  
- Adults reach up to **10 blocks in length** and **3 blocks in height** in‑game.  

---

## Behavior
- **Neutral**:  
  - Will retaliate when attacked.  
  - Often found in herds, reflecting nesting‑colony fossil evidence.  
  - Adults may linger near juveniles, showing protective tendencies.  

---

## Diet
- **Herbivore**  
- Feeds primarily on:  
  - ![](@jurassic-saga:gifs/leaves.gif) [**Leaves**](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Leaves)  
- In‑game, it grazes peacefully and wanders in open plains or forest edges.
