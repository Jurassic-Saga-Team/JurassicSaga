# Ranger Helicopter

The Ranger Helicopter is a military-style aerial transport vehicle designed for park ranger operations and rapid response situations.  
Based on the Bell 230 helicopter from *Jurassic Park: Operation Genesis*, it features a distinctive camouflage pattern and serves as a rugged alternative to the civilian Visitor Helicopter for patrolling dangerous areas and managing prehistoric creatures.

---

## Appearance
- Design inspired by the JPOG Ranger Helicopter (Bell 230)
- Military green camouflage pattern with yellow accents
- Yellow nose cone with park ranger insignia featuring a "P" logo
- Large windshield providing excellent visibility for pilot and passengers
- Rotating rotor blades during flight
- Retractable landing gear
- Robust military-style construction

---

## Functionality
- **Type**: Air vehicle
- **Capacity**: 5-6 passengers (including driver)
- **Speed**: Fast aerial movement
- **Fuel**: Not required
- **Durability**: 100 hearts (200 health points)
- **Special Features**: Vertical takeoff and landing, hover capability, retractable landing gear, high vantage point for spotting creatures

---

## Controls
- **Ascend**: Space
- **Forward/Backward**: W/S
- **Strafe Left/Right**: A/D
- **Toggle Landing Gear**: Z
- **Enter Seat**: Right-click on helicopter
- **Exit Vehicle**: Shift

## Usage Tips
- Use the Ranger Helicopter for patrolling remote areas and monitoring dangerous predators
- Perfect for park ranger operations and emergency evacuations
- Toggle landing gear with Z before landing to ensure a smooth touchdown
- The camouflage pattern helps blend into forested environments
- The helicopter's durability makes it vulnerable to large predators—avoid flying too low near hostile creatures
- Coordinate with passengers when entering and exiting to avoid accidents

## Trivia
- The Ranger Helicopter is directly inspired by the Bell 230 helicopter from *Jurassic Park: Operation Genesis*, where it was used for park management and ranger operations
- The military camouflage pattern distinguishes it from the civilian Visitor Helicopter, reflecting its role in more dangerous park operations
- The yellow nose cone with the park ranger "P" insignia is a direct reference to JPOG's ranger division branding
