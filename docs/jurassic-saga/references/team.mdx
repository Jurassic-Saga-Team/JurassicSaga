## Join the Team

We're always looking for talented individuals to join our development team!

### Current Openings

**We are actively seeking:**
- Block Modellers/Texturers (Primary need)
- Sound Designers
- Animal Texturers

**Requirements:**
- Your work must match our existing style for consistency
- Commitment to quality and franchise authenticity
- Ability to work collaboratively with the team

**We are NOT currently seeking:**
- Coders
- Animal Modellers

### How to Apply

Interested in contributing? Join our [Discord server](https://discord.gg/3b8k78f) and reach out to the development team with:
- Portfolio or examples of your work
- Your area of expertise
- Why you're interested in Jurassic Saga
