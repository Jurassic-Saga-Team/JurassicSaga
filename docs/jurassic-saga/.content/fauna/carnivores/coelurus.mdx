# Coelurus agilis

*Coelurus* (sɪˈljʊərəs, "si-LURE-əs") is a genus of small coelurosaurian dinosaur from the **Late Jurassic** of North America.  
Known for its lightweight build and long legs, it was likely a fast, agile predator or opportunistic scavenger.

---

## Appearance
- Slender, lightweight body built for speed
- Long legs and narrow torso, giving it a "shadow-through-the-brush" silhouette
- Small head with sharp teeth suited for catching small prey
- In-game size: **3 blocks long** and **2 blocks tall**

---

## Behavior 
- **Hostile (Requires 2)**: A single Coelurus will not attack the player; when **two or more** are present, they become aggressive and coordinate attacks
- Skittish when alone, bold in groups
- Prefers brushland, forest edges, and lightly wooded areas

---

## Obtaining
Coelurus does not spawn naturally. To obtain one, you must:
1. Find ![](@jurassic-saga:textures/item/natural/entity/animal/v2/extinct/coelurus/coelurus_fossil_remains) [**Coelurus Remains**](@jurassic-saga:fossil) or fossils
2. Use the fossil revival process to create a Coelurus
3. Hatch from an **Alligator Egg** type egg

---

## Diet
- **Carnivore**: feeds on <img src="https://i.imgur.com/4K6xsJA.gif" width={32} height={32} style={{imageRendering: "pixelated"}}/> meat sources
- Hunts small animals and scavenges when necessary

---

## Drops 
When defeated, Coelurus may drop: 
- ![](@jurassic-saga:textures/item/natural/entity/animal/v2/extinct/coelurus/coelurus_fossil_remains) [**Coelurus Remains**](@jurassic-saga:fossil)

## Trivia
- The achievement "The Brushland Shadow" references Coelurus's stealthy appearance and preference for brushland habitats
- The name *Coelurus* means "hollow tail," referring to its lightweight, hollow-boned skeleton
- Coelurus's pack behavior mechanic adds strategic depth, as lone individuals are harmless but groups become dangerous
