# Dracovenator regenti

*Dracovenator*, meaning "dragon hunter," is a genus of early theropod dinosaur from the **Early Jurassic** of South Africa.  
Closely related to Dilophosaurus, Dracovenator was a medium-sized predator that lived approximately 201–199 million years ago. It represents one of the earliest large predatory dinosaurs in Africa.

## Trivia
- The name *Dracovenator* means "dragon hunter," referencing the Drakensberg ("Dragon Mountains") region of South Africa where its fossils were discovered
- Dracovenator is closely related to Dilophosaurus from North America, suggesting a widespread distribution of early theropods
- As an Early Jurassic predator, Dracovenator lived during a time when dinosaurs were beginning to dominate terrestrial ecosystems
- The species name *regenti* honors the Regent, a South African ship that transported fossils to museums
