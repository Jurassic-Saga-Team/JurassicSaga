# Tylosaurus proriger

Tylosaurus was a massive marine reptile from the **Late Cretaceous period**, belonging to the mosasaur family.  
It could reach over **14 meters in length**, making it one of the largest mosasaurs.  
As a top predator, it fed on fish, other marine reptiles, and even smaller mosasaurs, dominating the ancient seas of North America.

---

## Appearance
- Long, streamlined body built for powerful swimming
- Narrow, elongated snout used for ramming and stunning prey
- Large, conical teeth ideal for gripping slippery marine animals
- Paddle-like limbs and a strong tail fluke for rapid propulsion
- In-game size: **18 blocks long** and **2 blocks tall**

---

## Behavior
- **Hostile**: Attacks players and most aquatic creatures on sight
- Uses high-speed lunges to ambush prey from below or behind
- Patrols deep waters and coastal regions, aggressively defending its territory

---

## Obtaining
Tylosaurus does not spawn naturally. To obtain one, you must:
1. Find ![](@jurassic-saga:textures/item/natural/entity/animal/v1/extinct/tylosaurus/tylosaurus_fossil_remains) [**Tylosaurus Remains**](@jurassic-saga:fossil) or fossils
2. Use the fossil revival process to create a Tylosaurus
3. Hatch from an **Alligator Egg** type egg

---

## Diet
- **Piscivore**: feeds primarily on <img src="https://i.imgur.com/qICGNC1.gif" width={32} height={32} style={{imageRendering: "pixelated"}}/> fish sources
- Hunts fish, squids, and other marine reptiles

---

## <img width={64} src="https://i.imgur.com/DTOgOBL.png" alt="Mr.DNA"/> Mr. DNA's Fun Trivia
- Tylosaurus was the 2nd largest mosasaur to ever live, only being beaten by it's cousin Mosasaurus itself!
- Tylosaurus preyed on prehistoric sharks, like Cretoxyrhina, and giant sea turtles, such as Protostega!
- The average adult Tylosaurus could reach 5 to 10 tons! You wouldn't want to ever see one right in front of you though!

---

## Trivia
- The achievement "Devil From The Deep" reflects Tylosaurus's role as a terrifying apex predator of ancient seas
- Tylosaurus was one of the largest mosasaurs, with some specimens estimated at over 14 meters (45 feet) in length
- Fossilized stomach contents show that Tylosaurus ate fish, sharks, plesiosaurs, and even other mosasaurs, making it a true oceanic apex predator
