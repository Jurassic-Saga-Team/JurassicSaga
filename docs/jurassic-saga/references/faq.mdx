What is Jurassic Saga?
Jurassic Saga aims to bring creatures from across the entire Jurassic Park franchise into your minecraft world, with a major focus on Park Building. 
The creatures, cosmetics and blocks featured in this mod hail not only from the films but also from the Novels, real life Toylines and various games from the Jurassic Park/World franchise.

Minecraft Version
Jurassic Saga is currently available on 1.21.1 (Neoforge and Fabric), and also in 1.20.1 (Forge and Fabric). We are not planning on downgrading past 1.20.1.

Requirements
Jurassic Saga requires Azurelib and Traveler's Tale

Pocket/Bedrock Edition ☠️
There is NOT and will NOT be a Pocket Edition/Bedrock Edition version of the mod, ever. Do not ask. Seriously.

Will creatures be accurate to the source material?
We aim to keep creatures close to their original appearances in the films, books, and games, but they won’t always be exact recreations. Some adjustments are made for consistency, game play needs, or design improvements. 

We also include original designs and concepts where material was lacking, especially for underrepresented species or ones who lack visuals.

Can I help with development?
Yes.
Currently we are looking for block modellers/texturers primarily. Sound makers and animal texturers are still welcome to apply. Our only criteria is that we want your work/style to match ours for consistency.

We are however not looking for coders or animal modellers.

I saw people playing with animals not in my version, how do I get them?
Some animals are given early access through the experimental config, bear in mind these animals have extremely basic AI. check the pinned comments in ⁠general to enable the experimental features.
