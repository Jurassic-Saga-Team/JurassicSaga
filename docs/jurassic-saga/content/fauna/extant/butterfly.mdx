# Butterfly

The *Butterfly* is a colorful flying insect found in various habitats worldwide.
Known for their vibrant wing patterns and role as pollinators, butterflies are beloved symbols of nature.
In-game, the butterfly is a tiny passive mob that adds beauty and ambient life to outdoor environments.

---

## Appearance
- Delicate insect with colorful, patterned wings
- Slender body with antennae
- Four large, scale-covered wings
- Graceful, fluttering flight pattern
- In-game size: **1 block long** and **1 block tall**

---

## Behavior
- **Passive**: Does not attack players or other mobs
- Flies gently in meandering patterns around flowers and plants
- Often found in meadows, forests, and gardens
- Adds peaceful atmosphere with graceful movements

---

## Obtaining
Butterfly spawns naturally in the overworld.

---

## Diet
- **Herbivore**: feeds on nectar from flowers
- Pollinates plants as it moves between flowers

---

## Drops
When defeated, Butterfly does not drop any items.

---
