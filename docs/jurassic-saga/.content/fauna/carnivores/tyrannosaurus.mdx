# Tyrannosaurus rex

Tyrannosaurus (meaning "tyrant lizard") was a massive theropod dinosaur that lived in **North America** during the **Late Cretaceous period**, around 73–66 million years ago.  
It is one of the most famous dinosaurs, known for its enormous skull, powerful jaws, and bone-crushing bite force.  
Tyrannosaurus was the apex predator of its ecosystem, capable of hunting large prey and scavenging when necessary.

---

## Appearance
- Massive, muscular theropod with a deep skull and powerful jaws
- Two-fingered forelimbs and long, heavy tail for balance
- Thick, robust legs built for short bursts of speed
- Large, forward-facing eyes providing strong depth perception
- In-game size: **17 blocks long** and **7 blocks tall**

---

## Behavior
- **Hostile**: Actively hunts players and most creatures on sight
- Uses **stalking** behavior to quietly approach prey before lunging
- Will **observe** targets from a distance before committing to an attack, adding unpredictability

---

## Obtaining
Tyrannosaurus does not spawn naturally. To obtain one, you must:
1. Find ![](@jurassic-saga:textures/item/natural/entity/animal/v1/extinct/tyrannosaurus/tyrannosaurus_fossil_remains) [**Tyrannosaurus Remains**](@jurassic-saga:fossil) or fossils
2. Use the fossil revival process to create a Tyrannosaurus
3. Hatch from an **Ostrich Egg** type egg

---

## Diet
- **Carnivore**: feeds on <img src="https://i.imgur.com/4K6xsJA.gif" width={32} height={32} style={{imageRendering: "pixelated"}}/> meat sources
- Hunts large animals and will scavenge from carcasses

---

## Drops
When defeated, Tyrannosaurus may drop:  
- ![](@jurassic-saga:textures/item/natural/entity/animal/v1/extinct/tyrannosaurus/tyrannosaurus_fossil_remains) [**Tyrannosaurus Remains**](@jurassic-saga:fossil)
- ![](@jurassic-saga:textures/item/natural/entity/animal/v1/extinct/tyrannosaurus/tyrannosaurus_raw) [**Tyrannosaurus Raw Meat**](@jurassic-saga:meats)

## Trivia
- The achievement "We Have A T-rex!" is a reference to Ian Malcolm's iconic line from *Jurassic Park* (1993): "You bred raptors?"
- The River cosmetic skin is a mixture of the novel description and Kenner's Electronic Tyrannosaurus<sup>[[2]](https://jurassicpark.fandom.com/wiki/Tyrannosaurus_rex/Novels#1995_Isla_Sorna_Expedition)</sup><sup>[[3]](https://jurassicpark.fandom.com/wiki/Electronic_Tyrannosaurus_rex)</sup>
- The Forest cosmetic skin is based on the Tyrannosaurus design from the Dino Crisis series<sup>[[4]](https://dinocrisis.fandom.com/wiki/Tyrannosaurus)</sup>
- Naming a Tyrannosaurus "Cyclop" through a nametag with the forest skin active will give you a special secret variant<sup>[[5]](https://dinocrisis.fandom.com/wiki/Tyrannosaurus#Noah's_Ark)</sup>
