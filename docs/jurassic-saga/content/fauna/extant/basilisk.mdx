# Basiliscus amoratus

*Basiliscus amoratus*, commonly known as the **brown basilisk**, is a species of lizard native to **Central America**.
It is famous for its ability to run across the surface of water, earning it the nickname "Jesus Christ lizard."
In-game, the Basilisk is a small, passive mob that adds atmosphere to tropical and river environments.

---

## Appearance
- Slender lizard with a long tail and agile limbs
- Distinctive crest along the back and head, used for display
- Brown-green coloration that blends into forest and riverside foliage
- In-game size: **2 blocks long** and **1 block tall**

---

## Behavior
- **Passive**: Flees quickly when approached, often running across water surfaces
- Frequently basks on rocks, logs, or riverbanks
- Avoids predators and players, relying on speed and agility

---

## Obtaining
Basilisk spawns naturally in Jungle Biomes.

---

## Diet
- **Insectivore**: feeds on insects and small invertebrates
- Hunts small prey along riverbanks and forest floors

---

## <img width={64} src="https://i.imgur.com/DTOgOBL.png" alt="Mr.DNA"/> Mr. DNA's Fun Trivia
- Basilisks are one of the few modern animals included in Jurassic Saga, serving as a source of Basilisk Eggs needed for hatching small dinosaurs.
- The real-world basilisk can run on water for short distances by rapidly slapping its feet on the surface, creating air pockets that prevent it from sinking.
- Basilisk Eggs are used to hatch small reptiles like Compsognathus and Microceratus.
