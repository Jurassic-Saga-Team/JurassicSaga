## Genetic Modifications

Genes are special items that can be applied to prehistoric creatures to alter their appearance, behavior, stats, or biological traits.  
Some genes provide cosmetic changes, while others grant powerful combat or survival bonuses.  
Not all creatures support every gene, and some genes may be stackable or have future planned functionality.

---

## Gene Categories

### Cosmetic Genes
Purely visual modifications.  
These genes change the **texture**, **pattern**, or **coloration** of a creature.

Examples include:
- **Arid Cosmetic**
- **Pasture Cosmetic**
- **Ocean Cosmetic**
- **Taiga Cosmetic**
- **Chaos Effect**, **Forest**, etc. (species‑specific)

---

## Functional Genes

Below is a list of all functional genes and their effects.

#### **Lux Gene**
- Adds a glow layer based on external lighting.  
- *Currently unimplemented.*

#### **Toxic Skin**
- When the creature is hit, the attacker receives **100 ticks of Wither**.

#### **Venom Bite**
- The creature’s melee attacks inflict **100 ticks of Wither**.

#### **Aggressive Instinct**
- Increases aggression level by **0.25%**.

#### **Bone Density**
- Grants **+10 Armor Toughness**  
- Grants **+10 Armor**

#### **Tooth Hardness**
- Adds **+8 Attack Damage**

#### **Skin Toughness**
- Grants **+5 Armor Toughness**  
- Grants **+20 Armor**

#### **Light Skeleton**
- Increases movement speed by **+0.25**

#### **Intensive Repair**
- Increases natural regeneration from **1 heart → 5 hearts**

#### **Macrosoma** *(stackable up to 4 times)*
- Increases creature model scale by **+0.1%** per application

#### **Microsoma** *(stackable up to 4 times)*
- Decreases creature model scale by **–0.1%** per application

#### **Gender Lean (Male/Female)**
- Forces the creature to become the specified gender

#### **Infertility**
- Prevents the creature from breeding

#### **Lysine Deficiency**
- *Unused.*  
- Planned: creatures will require lysine supplements to survive.

#### **Frequent Ovulation**
- Increases breeding frequency

#### **Parental Investment**
- Baby grows faster when near its parents

#### **Immune Response**
- *No current use.*

## Usage

- Genes are applied directly to a creature using the [**DNA Modifier**](@jurassic-saga:dna_modifier) (or equivalent mechanic in your mod).  
- Some genes can stack (e.g., **Macrosoma**, **Microsoma**).  
- Cosmetic genes override the creature’s default texture.  
- Functional genes modify stats immediately upon application.

---

## Notes & Limitations

- Some genes are **species‑restricted** and will not apply to all creatures.  
- Unimplemented genes will have no effect until future updates.  
- Genes cannot be removed once applied.  
- Applying multiple powerful genes may significantly alter balance.
