# Dimorphodon macronyx

*Dimorphodon* is an early Jurassic pterosaur known for its large, deep skull and heterodont dentition — two distinct tooth shapes in the same jaw. It lived along coastal cliffs and forested shorelines and represents one of the better‑known non‑pterodactyloid pterosaurs from Europe. 

---

## Appearance
- Large, bulky skull with strong jaws and two distinct tooth types (long fang‑like anterior teeth and smaller posterior teeth).   
- Shorter wings relative to body size compared with many later pterosaurs; robust limbs and long, stiffened tail.   
- Strong claws on hands and feet adapted for climbing and gripping perches.   
- In‑game size: **3 blocks long** and **1 block tall** (represents a compact body with a wingspan scaled down from fossil estimates).

---

## Behavior
- **Hostile**: Will attack players if provoked or when defending territory.  
- Likely a perch‑and‑pounce hunter that used strong limbs and claws to climb and launch into short, powerful flights rather than long soaring.   
- Hunts small vertebrates and large invertebrates; may scavenge opportunistically.   
- When threatened, Dimorphodon may attempt to flee to higher ground or cling to vertical surfaces using its claws.

## Trivia
- The name *Dimorphodon* means **"two‑form tooth"**, referring to its unusual heterodont dentition.   
- The species *macronyx* means **"large claw"**, a nod to its prominent manual and pedal claws.   
- The first specimens were discovered in the Lyme Regis area of southern England in the early 19th century; the discovery helped establish pterosaurs as a distinct group of flying reptiles.   
- Adults reached a wingspan of roughly **1.4–1.5 metres**, making Dimorphodon a compact but powerful flier among early Jurassic pterosaurs.
