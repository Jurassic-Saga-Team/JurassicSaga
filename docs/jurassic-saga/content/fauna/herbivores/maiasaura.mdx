# Maiasaura peeblesorum

*Maiasaura* is a hadrosaurid dinosaur famous for its reputation as the **"good mother lizard"**.
Fossil evidence shows that Maiasaura cared for its young in nesting colonies, returning to the same nesting grounds year after year.
Lightweight for its size and capable of running, Maiasaura was a social, herd-dwelling herbivore of the Late Cretaceous.

---

## Appearance
- Medium-sized hadrosaur with a long tail and sturdy hind limbs
- Rounded snout and beak adapted for cropping vegetation
- Smooth, streamlined body typical of hadrosaurids
- In-game size: **10 blocks long** and **3 blocks tall**

---

## Behavior
- **Neutral**: Will not attack unless provoked
- Retaliates when attacked
- Often found in herds, reflecting nesting-colony fossil evidence
- Adults may linger near juveniles, showing protective tendencies

---

## Obtaining
Maiasaura does not spawn naturally. To obtain one, you must:
1. Find ![](@jurassic-saga:textures/item/natural/entity/animal/v2/extinct/maiasaura/maiasaura_fossil_remains) [**Maiasaura Remains**](@jurassic-saga:fossil) or fossils
2. Use the fossil revival process to create a Maiasaura
3. Hatch from an **Alligator Egg** type egg

---

## Diet
- **Herbivore**: feeds primarily on <img src="https://i.imgur.com/INKBvsq.gif" width={32} height={32} style={{imageRendering: "pixelated"}}/> [**Leaves**](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Leaves)
- Grazes peacefully and wanders in open plains or forest edges

---

## Drops
When defeated, Maiasaura may drop:
- ![](@jurassic-saga:textures/item/natural/entity/animal/v2/extinct/maiasaura/maiasaura_fossil_remains) [**Maiasaura Remains**](@jurassic-saga:fossil)
- ![](@jurassic-saga:textures/item/natural/entity/animal/v2/extinct/maiasaura/maiasaura_raw) [**Maiasaura Raw Meat**](@jurassic-saga:meats)

## <img width={64} src="https://i.imgur.com/DTOgOBL.png" alt="Mr.DNA"/> Mr. DNA's Fun Trivia
- The name *Maiasaura* means "good mother lizard," referencing the discovery of extensive nesting grounds with fossilized eggs, hatchlings, and adults together.
- The achievement "Good mother lizard" is a direct translation of the dinosaur's name, honoring its nurturing behavior.
