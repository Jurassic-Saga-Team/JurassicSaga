# Procompsognathus triassicus

Procompsognathus was a small, lightly built dinosaur from the **Late Triassic period** of Europe, with fossils discovered in Germany.  
It was a fast runner and likely insectivorous or carnivorous, feeding on insects and small vertebrates.  
Its small size and speed made it effective at darting through underbrush, and it may have lived in groups for protection.

---

## Appearance
- Small, slender theropod with a lightweight frame.  
- Long legs adapted for quick bursts of speed.  
- Narrow snout with small, sharp teeth suited for catching insects and tiny prey.  
- Adults reach up to **2 blocks in length** and **1 block in height** in‑game.  

---

## Behavior
- **Hostile (Requires 3)**:  
  - A single Procompsognathus will not attack the player.  
  - When **three or more** are present, they become aggressive and swarm their target.  
  - Uses quick darting movements to harass prey and overwhelm through numbers.  

---

## Diet
- **Carnivore**  
- Feeds primarily on:  
  - ![](@jurassic-saga:gifs/carnivore_edible.gif) **Meats**  
- In‑game, it hunts small creatures and scavenges scraps left by larger predators.
