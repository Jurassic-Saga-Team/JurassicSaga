# Carcharodontosaurus saharicus

*Carcharodontosaurus*, meaning **"shark-toothed lizard,"** is a genus of massive carcharodontosaurid theropod dinosaur containing two known species, with *Carcharodontosaurus saharicus* being the most well-known.
It lived during the **Late Cretaceous** of North Africa and was one of the largest carnivorous dinosaurs to ever walk the Earth, rivaling even Tyrannosaurus and Spinosaurus in size.

---

## Appearance
- Enormous theropod with a deep, elongated skull filled with serrated, blade-like teeth
- Relatively small arms compared to its massive body, similar in proportion to other large theropods
- Powerfully built hind limbs supporting its immense weight
- In-game size: **21 blocks long** and **6 blocks tall**, making it one of the largest predators in Jurassic Saga

---

## Behavior
- **Hostile**: Will attack players and most mobs on sight
- Extremely aggressive apex predator, chasing prey over long distances
- Due to its enormous size and health pool, it is one of the most dangerous creatures in the mod

---

## Obtaining
Carcharodontosaurus does not spawn naturally. To obtain one, you must:
1. Enable [Experimental Features]($references/experimental)
2. Find ![](@jurassic-saga:textures/item/natural/entity/animal/v5_5/extinct/carcharodontosaurus/carcharodontosaurus_fossil_remains) [**Carcharodontosaurus Remains**](@jurassic-saga:fossil) or fossils
3. Use the fossil revival process to create a Carcharodontosaurus
4. Hatch from an **Ostrich Egg** type egg

## <img width={64} src="https://i.imgur.com/DTOgOBL.png" alt="Mr.DNA"/> Mr. DNA's Fun Trivia
- Carcharodontosaurus was the only other predator able to compete with Spinosaurus in their ecosystem!
- Carcharodontosaurus' name can actually mean both Sharp-Toothed Lizard and Shark-Toothed Lizard due to how Carcharodon, being the genus of Great White Sharks, means sharp-toothed itself!
- Carcharodontosaurus was one of the largest dinosaurs to ever exist, being only behind Spinosaurus, Tyrannosaurus and some of it's cousins such as Giganotosaurus and Mapusaurus!
- Carcharodontosaurus was discovered in early April 1914 by Austro-Hungarian paleontologist Richard Markgraf. It would later be described by German paleontologist Ernst Stromer, the same person who discovered Spinosaurus two years prior.
- Carcharodontosaurus's appearance in Jurassic Saga is based on it's depiction seen in the 2003 video game Jurassic Park: Operation Genesis
- Carcharodontosaurus's translated name is often misinterpreted as "Shark-toothed" due to being named after the genus name of the Great White Shark (Carcharodon carcharias)
