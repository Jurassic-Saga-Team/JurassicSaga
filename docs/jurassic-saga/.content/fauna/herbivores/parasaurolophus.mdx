# Parasaurolophus cyrtocristatus

A herbivorous dinosaur from the **Late Cretaceous**, known for its long, backward‑curving crest.  
The crest may have been used for **visual display**, **sound resonance**, or **thermoregulation**.  
Parasaurolophus likely lived in herds and fed on a variety of vegetation.

---

## Appearance
- Large hadrosaur with a long, sweeping cranial crest.  
- Slender body with strong hind limbs and a balancing tail.  
- Smooth, rounded snout typical of hadrosaurids.  
- Adults reach up to **12 blocks in length** and **5 blocks in height** in‑game.  

---

## Behavior
- **Neutral**:  
  - Will retaliate when attacked.  
  - Often found in herds, using group movement for safety.  
  - Uses its crest to produce low‑frequency calls that alert nearby herd members.  

---

## Diet
- **Herbivore**  
- Feeds primarily on:  
  - ![](@jurassic-saga:gifs/leaves.gif) [**Leaves**](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Leaves)  
- In‑game, it grazes peacefully in open plains and forest edges.  

---
