# Gallimimus bullatus

Gallimimus was a fast, ostrich-like dinosaur from the **Late Cretaceous period**, discovered in Mongolia.  
It had long legs adapted for speed and a toothless beak, suggesting a varied diet.  
Fossil evidence indicates Gallimimus may have moved in large groups, potentially for protection or migration.

---

## Appearance
- Long, slender legs built for high-speed running
- Toothless beak suited for omnivorous or herbivorous feeding
- Lightweight body with a long tail for balance
- In-game size: **6 blocks long** and **4 blocks tall**

---

## Behavior
- **Neutral**: Will not attack unless provoked
- Flees when attacked or startled
- Often found in **large herds**, reflecting fossil evidence of group behavior
- Extremely fast, capable of outrunning most predators

---

## Obtaining
Gallimimus does not spawn naturally. To obtain one, you must:
1. Find ![](@jurassic-saga:textures/item/natural/entity/animal/v1/extinct/gallimimus/gallimimus_fossil_remains) [**Gallimimus Remains**](@jurassic-saga:fossil) or fossils
2. Use the fossil revival process to create a Gallimimus
3. Hatch from an **Alligator Egg** type egg

---

## Diet
- **Herbivore**: feeds primarily on <img src="https://i.imgur.com/INKBvsq.gif" width={32} height={32} style={{imageRendering: "pixelated"}}/> [**Leaves**](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Leaves)
- Grazes on vegetation and wanders in open plains or forest edges

---

## Drops
When defeated, Gallimimus may drop:  
- ![](@jurassic-saga:textures/item/natural/entity/animal/v1/extinct/gallimimus/gallimimus_fossil_remains) [**Gallimimus Remains**](@jurassic-saga:fossil)
- ![](@jurassic-saga:textures/item/natural/entity/animal/v1/extinct/gallimimus/gallimimus_raw) [**Gallimimus Raw Meat**](@jurassic-saga:meats)
