# Triceratops horridus

Triceratops was a large, horned herbivore from the **Late Cretaceous period**, living in North America at the very end of the age of dinosaurs.  
It is instantly recognizable by its three facial horns and broad frill, features that likely served both defensive and display purposes.  
Triceratops lived in herds and fed on low‑lying vegetation, making it one of the most iconic ceratopsids.

---

## Appearance
- Large skull with three horns — two above the eyes and one on the nose.  
- Broad frill extending from the back of the head.  
- Stocky body supported by strong, pillar‑like legs.  
- In‑game size: up to **11 blocks in length** and **5 blocks in height**.  

---

## Behavior
- **Neutral**: Triceratops does not attack players unless provoked.  
- Moves steadily in herds, grazing on vegetation.  
- May display defensive postures, reflecting its protective nature.  

---

## Diet
- **Herbivore** — feeds primarily on [Leaves](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Leaves).  
- Browses on low‑lying plants and shrubs.  

---

## Health
- **Adult**: 90 health (x45 hearts)
