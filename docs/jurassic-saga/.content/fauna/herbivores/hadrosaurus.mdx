# Hadrosaurus foulkii

Hadrosaurus was a duck‑billed herbivore from the **Late Cretaceous period** of North America.  
Known for its strong hind legs and broad, flattened snout, it likely moved in herds and may have used vocalizations to communicate.  
As one of the earliest hadrosaurids described, Hadrosaurus represents a key member of the “duck‑billed” dinosaurs.

---

## Appearance
- Broad, flattened snout resembling a duck’s bill.  
- Strong hind legs adapted for both walking and running.  
- Long tail used for balance.  
- In‑game size: up to **11 blocks in length** and **5 blocks in height**.  

---

## Behavior
- **Neutral**: Hadrosaurus does not attack players unless provoked.  
- Moves steadily in herds, grazing on vegetation.  
- May rest or vocalize, reflecting its social nature.  

---

## Diet
- **Herbivore** — feeds primarily on [Leaves](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Leaves).  
- Browses on mid‑level vegetation, consistent with its duck‑bill snout.  

---

## Health
- **Adult**: 80 health (40 hearts)
