# Syringe

The Syringe is a reusable tool essential for genetic engineering and DNA extraction.  
Used to extract genes from creatures, clean eggs, and fertilize seeds, the Syringe is a fundamental item for creating prehistoric life in Jurassic Saga.

---

## Functionality

- **Type**: Reusable tool
- **Durability**: Infinite (never breaks or runs out)
- **States**: Clean or Dirty
- **Primary Uses**:
  - Extract genes from living creatures
  - Clean eggs for fertilization
  - Fertilize eggs with DNA
  - Fertilize seeds with Plant DNA

---

## Usage

### Extracting Genes

Right-click on any vanilla mob with a **Clean Syringe** to extract their associated gene:
1. Approach a mob that has an extractable gene
2. Right-click the mob with a Clean Syringe
3. The syringe extracts the gene and becomes dirty
4. The extracted gene is added to your inventory

For a complete list of genes and their sources, see the [**Genes**]($content/items/genes) page.

### Cleaning Eggs

Used in crafting to prepare eggs for fertilization:
- **Input**: Creature Egg + Clean Syringe
- **Output**: Unfertilized Egg (egg becomes ready for DNA fertilization)
- **Result**: Syringe becomes dirty after crafting

### Fertilizing Eggs

Used in crafting to create fertilized eggs:
- **Input**: DNA + Unfertilized Egg + Clean Syringe
- **Output**: Fertilized Egg (ready for incubation)
- **Result**: Syringe becomes dirty after crafting

For detailed instructions, see [**Creating an Egg**]($guides/creating_an_egg).

### Fertilizing Seeds

Used in crafting to create fertilized plant seeds:
- **Input**: Plant DNA + Unfertilized Seeds + Clean Syringe
- **Output**: Fertilized Plant Seeds (ready for cultivation)
- **Result**: Syringe becomes dirty after crafting

For detailed instructions, see [**Creating a Plant**]($guides/plant_creation).

---

## Cleaning

After use in crafting or gene extraction, the Syringe becomes dirty and must be cleaned before reuse:

1. Hold the **Dirty Syringe** in your hand
2. Throw on any water source (ocean, river, lake, or water block, cauldrons, half slabs with waterlogged)
3. The syringe instantly becomes clean and ready for reuse

**Important:** Dirty syringes cannot be used for any crafting recipes or gene extraction until cleaned.

## Tips

- Keep multiple syringes on hand for efficient genetic work
- Clean syringes immediately after use to avoid confusion
- Syringes never break or run out, making them a permanent investment
- Any water source works for cleaning  rivers, oceans, or even a single water block
- Plan your gene extraction trips by identifying which mobs you need before heading out

---

## Trivia

- The Syringe is inspired by the genetic extraction equipment seen throughout the Jurassic Park franchise
- Unlike most tools in Minecraft, syringes never degrade or break, reflecting their role as precision laboratory equipment
- The cleaning mechanic adds a layer of realism to the genetic engineering process
- Syringes are one of the most versatile tools in Jurassic Saga, used in nearly every stage of creature creation
