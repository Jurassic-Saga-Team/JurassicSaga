# Bananogmius aratus

*Bananogmius* is a genus of small, laterally compressed freshwater fish from the **Late Cretaceous** of North America.  
Known for its deep, rounded body and schooling behavior, Bananogmius occupied freshwater and nearshore environments and is a common component of Cretaceous fish assemblages.

---

## Appearance
- Small, deep‑bodied fish with a laterally compressed silhouette  
- Rounded dorsal profile and a narrow caudal peduncle leading to a forked tail  
- Likely displayed bright or contrasting patterns in life for schooling cohesion or camouflage  
- Delicate fins and a small mouth adapted for feeding on plankton and small invertebrates  
- In‑game size: **1 block long** and **1 block tall**

---

## Behavior
- **Passive**: non‑aggressive and will not attack players or other passive creatures  
- Frequently found in schools; grouping reduces predation risk  
- Prefers calm waters such as lakes, slow rivers, and sheltered bays  
- Easily preyed upon by larger aquatic predators and wading creatures

---

## Obtaining
Bananogmius does not spawn naturally. To obtain one, you must:

4. Hatch from a **Fish Egg** type egg

## Trivia
- The name *Bananogmius* loosely evokes the fish’s curved, deep body shape rather than any relation to bananas  
- Bananogmius is commonly found in Late Cretaceous freshwater deposits and helps reconstruct ancient inland ecosystems  
- In Jurassic Saga, the achievement **"Banana Fish"** is a lighthearted nod to the genus name and its small, distinctive silhouette
