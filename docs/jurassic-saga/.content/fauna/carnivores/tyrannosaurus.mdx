# Tyrannosaurus rex

Tyrannosaurus (meaning "tyrant lizard") was a massive theropod dinosaur that lived in **North America** during the **Late Cretaceous period**, around 73–66 million years ago.  
It is one of the most famous dinosaurs, known for its enormous skull, powerful jaws, and bone‑crushing bite force.  
Tyrannosaurus was the apex predator of its ecosystem, capable of hunting large prey and scavenging when necessary.

---

## Appearance
- Enormous skull with powerful jaws and serrated teeth.  
- Short but muscular arms with two claws.  
- Massive hind limbs supporting its heavy body.  
- Long, heavy tail for balance.  
- In‑game size: up to **17 blocks in length** and **7 blocks in height**.  

---

## Behavior
- **Hostile**: Tyrannosaurus will attack players and mobs on sight.  
- Apex predator behavior, hunts large creatures and defends its territory aggressively.  
- May roam alone, reflecting its solitary nature.  

---

## Diet
- **Carnivore**: feeds on meat sources.  
- Capable of hunting large prey or scavenging remains.  

---

## Health
- **Adult**: 120 health (x60 hearts)
