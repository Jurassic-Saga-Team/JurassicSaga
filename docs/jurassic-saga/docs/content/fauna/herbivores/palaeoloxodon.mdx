*Palaeoloxodon*, meaning **"ancient straight-tusked elephant,"** is a genus of extinct elephant from the **Pleistocene** of Europe and Asia.  
The dwarf island form, *P. falconeri*, is one of the most extreme examples of insular dwarfism ever recorded  shrinking from a mainland ancestor over 4 meters tall down to less than 1 meter at the shoulder.

---

## Appearance
- Compact, heavily built elephant with four stout pillar-like legs and a broad rectangular body
- Short, downward-curved trunk compared to modern elephants
- Both sexes share the same uniform medium grey coat with subtle darker shading across the back and sides
- **Male**: Features a prominent pair of long, forward-sweeping ivory-white tusks
- **Female**: Tuskless, otherwise identical in build and coloration to the male
- In-game size: **2 blocks long** and **1 block tall**

---

## Behavior
- **Neutral**: Will not attack unless provoked; once agitated, will charge and attack the offending player or mob
- Despite its small stature, it is surprisingly aggressive when threatened  do not underestimate it
- Moves in a slow, plodding gait while passive

---

## Obtaining
Palaeoloxodon does not spawn naturally. To obtain one, you must:
1. Find ![](@jurassic-saga:textures/item/natural/entity/animal/misc_other/palaeoloxodon/palaeoloxodon_fossil_remains) [**Palaeoloxodon Remains**]($content/items/fossil) or fossils
2. Use the fossil revival process to create a Palaeoloxodon
3. Born from a **Gestation Chamber** (not yet possible)

---

## Diet
- **Herbivore**: feeds on <img src="https://i.imgur.com/INKBvsq.gif" width={32} height={32} style={{imageRendering: "pixelated"}}/> leaves, grasses, and plant matter
