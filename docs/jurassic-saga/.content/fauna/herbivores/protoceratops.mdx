# Protoceratops hellenikorhinus

*Protoceratops* is a genus of small protoceratopsid dinosaurs that lived in Asia during the **Late Cretaceous**, around 75–71 million years ago.  
The genus includes two species: *P. andrewsi* and the larger *P. hellenikorhinus*.  
It is often depicted locked in combat with *Velociraptor*, based on a famous fossil find.

---

## Appearance
- Small ceratopsian with a short frill and beaked mouth
- Robust body and strong limbs for digging or foraging
- Lacked horns but had a characteristic frill shape unique to the genus
- In-game size: **5 blocks long** and **2 blocks tall**

---

## Behavior
- **Neutral**: Will not attack unless provoked
- Retaliates when attacked
- Often found in small groups, reflecting herd-like behavior
- Uses its beak and body weight to defend itself when cornered

---

## Obtaining
Protoceratops does not spawn naturally. To obtain one, you must:
1. Find ![](@jurassic-saga:textures/item/natural/entity/animal/v2/extinct/protoceratops/protoceratops_fossil_remains) [**Protoceratops Remains**](@jurassic-saga:fossil) or fossils
2. Use the fossil revival process to create a Protoceratops
3. Hatch from a **Chicken Egg** type egg

---

## Diet
- **Herbivore**: feeds primarily on <img src="https://i.imgur.com/INKBvsq.gif" width={32} height={32} style={{imageRendering: "pixelated"}}/> [**Leaves**](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Leaves)
- Grazes on shrubs and low vegetation

## <img width={64} src="https://i.imgur.com/DTOgOBL.png" alt="Mr.DNA"/> Mr. DNA's Fun Trivia
- The achievement "Locked In Battle" references the famous "Fighting Dinosaurs" fossil discovered in 1971, which preserved a Protoceratops and Velociraptor locked in mortal combat during a sandstorm
- Protoceratops is one of the most well-known early ceratopsians and was among the first dinosaurs discovered in Mongolia's Gobi Desert
