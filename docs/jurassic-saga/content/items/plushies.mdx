# Plushies

Plushies are decorative placeable items featuring chibi-style versions of creatures from Jurassic Saga.
They can be placed in the world like small objects and serve a purely cosmetic purpose.

<Callout variant="warning">
    Plushies are currently only obtainable in Creative mode and cannot be crafted or found in Survival.
</Callout>
