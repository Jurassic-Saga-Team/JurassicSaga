# Callovosaurus leedsi

*Callovosaurus* (meaning **"Callovian lizard"**) is a genus of iguanodontian dinosaur known from Middle Jurassic rocks of England.
It is one of the earliest known dryosaurids and represents a small, agile herbivore that lived in forested environments.

---

## Appearance
- Small, lightweight ornithopod with a slender build
- Long tail used for balance while running
- Narrow head with a beak suited for cropping vegetation
- In-game size: **4 blocks long** and **1 block tall**

---

## Behavior
- **Passive**: Will flee when attacked or startled
- Often found grazing in small groups
- Prefers open forests and lightly wooded areas

---

## Obtaining
Callovosaurus does not spawn naturally. To obtain one, you must:
1. Find ![](@jurassic-saga:textures/item/natural/entity/animal/v2/extinct/callovosaurus/callovosaurus_fossil_remains) [**Callovosaurus Remains**](@jurassic-saga:fossil) or fossils
2. Use the fossil revival process to create a Callovosaurus
3. Hatch from an **Alligator Egg** type egg

---

## Diet
- **Herbivore**: feeds primarily on <img src="https://i.imgur.com/INKBvsq.gif" width={32} height={32} style={{imageRendering: "pixelated"}}/> [**Leaves**](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Leaves)
- Wanders and grazes peacefully in forested areas

---

## Drops
When defeated, Callovosaurus may drop:
- ![](@jurassic-saga:textures/item/natural/entity/animal/v2/extinct/callovosaurus/callovosaurus_fossil_remains) [**Callovosaurus Remains**](@jurassic-saga:fossil)
- ![](@jurassic-saga:textures/item/natural/entity/animal/v2/extinct/callovosaurus/callovosaurus_raw) [**Callovosaurus Raw Meat**](@jurassic-saga:meats)

## <img width={64} src="https://i.imgur.com/DTOgOBL.png" alt="Mr.DNA"/> Mr. DNA's Fun Trivia
- Callovosaurus is one of the earliest known Iguanodontians, possibly being related to Dryomorphs like Dryosaurus and Cumnoria!
- Callovosaurus lived in what is now the Oxford Clay Formation, alongside animals like Loricatosaurus and Sarcolestes.
- Callovosaurus' name, "Callovian Lizard", comes from the Callovian stage of the Jurassic period, of which it was alive and present on!
