# Hadrosaurus foulkii

Hadrosaurus was a duck‑billed herbivore from the **Late Cretaceous period** of North America.  
Known for its strong hind legs and broad, flattened snout, it likely moved in herds and may have used vocalizations to communicate.  
As one of the earliest hadrosaurids described, Hadrosaurus represents a key member of the “duck‑billed” dinosaurs.

---

## Appearance
- Large, robust hadrosaur with a long tail and powerful hind limbs.
- Broad, flat beak used for cropping vegetation.
- Smooth, rounded body shape typical of early hadrosaurids.
- Adults reach up to **11 blocks in length** and **4 blocks in height** in‑game.

---

## Behavior
- **Neutral**:  
  - Will retaliate when attacked.  
  - Often found in herds, reflecting real‑world social behavior.  
  - Uses vocalizations to alert nearby herd members of danger.  

---

## Diet
- **Herbivore**  
- Feeds primarily on:

- In‑game, it grazes peacefully in open plains and forest edges.
