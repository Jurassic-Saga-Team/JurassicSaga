# Jeep Wrangler

| | |

| **Speed** | Fast |
| **Fuel** | Not Required |

| **Craftable** | Not yet implemented |
| **Obtained** | Creative Mode Only |


The Jeep Wrangler is a rugged all-terrain vehicle designed for exploring the island's diverse environments.  
Inspired by the iconic Jeep Wrangler from *Jurassic Park* (1993), this vehicle features a distinctive red and gray color scheme that fans will recognize.

---

## Appearance
- Design inspired by the Jeep Wrangler from *Jurassic Park* (1993)
- Red body with gray lower panels
- Roll cage with roof-mounted lights
- Large off-road tires with red rims
- Comes without a roof by default; soft-top cover can be attached
- Clean styling without branded logos (for copyright compliance)

---

## Functionality
- **Type**: Ground vehicle
- **Capacity**: 4-5 passengers (including driver)
- **Speed**: Fast off-road and on-road movement
- **Fuel**: Not required
- **Durability**: 100 hearts (200 health points)
- **Special Features**: Attachable roof cover for weather protection, excellent off-road capability, headlights

---

- **Forward/Backward**: W/S
- **Turn Left/Right**: A/D
- **Enter Seat**: Right-click on Jeep

## Usage Tips
- Perfect for exploring rugged terrain and navigating through forests
- The Jeep spawns without a roof, providing open-air driving and excellent visibility
- Attach a roof cover for protection from rain or when extra cover is needed
- The Jeep's compact size allows it to maneuver through tight spaces between trees
- Excellent for quick escapes from predators thanks to its speed and agility
- The roll cage provides some protection, but avoid confrontations with large carnivores
- Coordinate with passengers when entering and exiting to ensure everyone's safety

## Trivia
- The Jeep Wrangler is inspired by one of the most iconic vehicles from *Jurassic Park* (1993), used for the park tour
- The red and gray color scheme pays homage to the original film's tour vehicles
- The Jeep spawns without a roof, just like the open-air tour vehicles in the film, with the option to add one later
- For copyright reasons, the vehicle does not feature branded logos or vehicle numbers, but the distinctive color scheme is instantly recognizable to fans
