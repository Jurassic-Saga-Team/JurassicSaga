# Velociraptor mongoliensis

Velociraptor was a small, agile predator from the **Late Cretaceous period**, with fossils discovered in Mongolia and China.  
It measured around 2 meters in length and was covered in feathers, though it could not fly.  
Velociraptor is best known for its sickle-shaped claw on each foot, which it likely used to pin down prey.  
It may have hunted small animals or scavenged, and evidence suggests it could have worked in packs to take down larger prey.

---

## Appearance
- Small, feathered theropod with a sleek, agile build
- Large, forward-facing eyes providing excellent depth perception
- Prominent sickle claw on each foot used for gripping prey
- In-game size: **5 blocks long** and **2 blocks tall**

---

## Behavior
- **Hostile**: Actively attacks players and small creatures on sight
- Hunts in coordinated packs, circling and flanking targets
- Uses rapid leaps and slashing attacks to overwhelm prey

---

## Obtaining
Velociraptor does not spawn naturally. To obtain one, you must:
1. Find ![](@jurassic-saga:textures/item/natural/entity/animal/v1/extinct/velociraptor/velociraptor_fossil_remains) [**Velociraptor Remains**](@jurassic-saga:fossil) or fossils
2. Use the fossil revival process to create a Velociraptor
3. Hatch from an **Alligator Egg** type egg

---

## Diet
- **Carnivore**: feeds on <img src="https://i.imgur.com/4K6xsJA.gif" width={32} height={32} style={{imageRendering: "pixelated"}}/> meat sources
- Hunts small to medium animals and scavenges when needed

---

## Drops
When defeated, Velociraptor may drop:  
- ![](@jurassic-saga:textures/item/natural/entity/animal/v1/extinct/velociraptor/velociraptor_fossil_remains) [**Velociraptor Remains**](@jurassic-saga:fossil)
- ![](@jurassic-saga:textures/item/natural/entity/animal/v1/extinct/velociraptor/velociraptor_raw) [**Velociraptor Raw Meat**](@jurassic-saga:meats)

## <img width={64} src="https://i.imgur.com/DTOgOBL.png" alt="Mr.DNA"/> Mr. DNA's Fun Trivia
- Velociraptor preyed on Protoceratops, with the fossil titled "Fighting Dinosaurs" containing the preserved skeletons of a Protoceratops and Velociraptor fighting!
- Velociraptor could run as fast as up to 25 mph (40 km/h), justifying it's name: "Swift Thief"!
- Velociraptor lived in what is now the Gobi Desert alongside dinosaurs like Oviraptor, Protoceratops and Pinacosaurus!
