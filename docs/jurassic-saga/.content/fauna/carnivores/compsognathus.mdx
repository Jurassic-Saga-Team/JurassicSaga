# Compsognathus longipes

*Compsognathus* is a genus of small, bipedal, carnivorous theropod dinosaur.  
Members of its single species, *Compsognathus longipes*, grew to roughly the size of a chicken, making them one of the smallest known non‑avian dinosaurs.

---

## Appearance
- Small, slender body built for quick darting movements.  
- Long tail used for balance while running.  
- Narrow snout with small, sharp teeth suited for catching insects and tiny prey.  
- In‑game size: **1 block long**, **1 block tall**.  

---

## Behavior 
- **Hostile (Requires 3)**:  
  - A single Compsognathus will **not** attack the player.  
  - When **three or more** are present, they become aggressive and swarm the target.  
- Highly social and often found in groups.  
- Quick and evasive, making them tricky to hit despite their low health.  

---

## Diet
- **Carnivore**  
- Feeds on ![](@jurassic-saga:gifs/carnivore_edible.gif) meat sources.  
- Hunts small animals and scavenges scraps left by larger predators.
