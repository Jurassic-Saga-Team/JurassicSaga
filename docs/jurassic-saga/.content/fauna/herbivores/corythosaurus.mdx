# Corythosaurus casuarius

*Corythosaurus* is a genus of hadrosaurid “duck‑billed” dinosaur from the **Late Cretaceous**, living around 77–75.7 million years ago in what is now western North America.
It is best known for its tall, helmet‑shaped crest, which likely played a role in communication and display.

---

## Appearance
- Large hadrosaur with a tall, rounded crest resembling a Corinthian helmet.
- Long, muscular tail used for balance while moving.
- Thick hind legs for walking and running; smaller forelimbs for foraging.
- Scaly skin with subtle striping or patterning depending on cosmetic genes.
- In‑game size: **12 blocks long** and **4 blocks tall**.

---

## Behavior
- **Neutral**: 
    - Will not attack unless provoked.
    - Flees when threatened, often calling out to nearby herd members.
- Moves in small herds and prefers open plains or forest edges.
- Occasionally rears up to reach higher vegetation.
- Uses vocalizations to communicate with other Corythosaurus nearby.

---

## Diet
- **Herbivore**: feeds primarily on <img src="https://i.imgur.com/INKBvsq.gif" width={32} height={32} style={{imageRendering: "pixelated"}}/> [**Leaves**](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Leaves).  
- May also graze on low shrubs, ferns, and other plant blocks depending on availability.
