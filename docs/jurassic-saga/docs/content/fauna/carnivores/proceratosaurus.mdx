*Proceratosaurus* is a genus of small theropod dinosaur that lived during the **Middle Jurassic** of England.  
It is known from a mostly complete skull, showing a distinctive nasal crest that hints at its evolutionary ties to later tyrannosauroids.

---

## Appearance
- Small, lightly built theropod with long legs for quick pursuit
- Distinctive nasal crest rising from the snout
- Narrow jaws lined with sharp, serrated teeth
- In-game size: **5 blocks long** and **2 blocks tall**

---

## Behavior
- **Neutral**: Will not attack unless provoked
- Retaliates when attacked
- Hunts small animals but avoids larger predators
- Often found alone or in pairs, patrolling forest edges

---

## Obtaining
Proceratosaurus does not spawn naturally. To obtain one, you must:
1. Find ![](@jurassic-saga:textures/item/natural/entity/animal/v2/extinct/proceratosaurus/proceratosaurus_fossil_remains) [**Proceratosaurus Remains**]($content/items/fossil) or fossils
2. Use the fossil revival process to create a Proceratosaurus
3. Hatch from a **Chicken Egg** type egg

---

## Diet
- **Carnivore**: feeds on <img src="https://i.imgur.com/4K6xsJA.gif" width={32} height={32} style={{imageRendering: "pixelated"}}/> meat sources
- Hunts small creatures and scavenges when necessary

---

## Drops
When defeated, Proceratosaurus may drop:  
- ![](@jurassic-saga:textures/item/natural/entity/animal/v2/extinct/proceratosaurus/proceratosaurus_fossil_remains) [**Proceratosaurus Remains**]($content/items/fossil)
- ![](@jurassic-saga:textures/item/natural/entity/animal/v2/extinct/proceratosaurus/proceratosaurus_raw) [**Proceratosaurus Raw Meat**]($content/items/meats)

## <img width={64} src="https://i.imgur.com/DTOgOBL.png" alt="Mr.DNA"/> Mr. DNA's Fun Trivia
- Proceratosaurus lived in the Middle Jurassic period in what is now England, being one of the earliest known Tyrannosauroids, the same superfamily the Late Cretaceous Tyrannosaurus belonged to!
- Originally, Proceratosaurus was named for it's supposed close relationship with Ceratosaurus, due to an incomplete part of it's head being misinterpreted as a horn, which we now know would've likely been part of a crest, akin to the one present in Guanlong, a close relative of Proceratosaurus!
- Proceratosaurus lived alongside animals like Cetiosaurus and Cardiodon, in what is now the Forest Marble Formation of England!
- The achievement "Clever Little Hunter" reflects Proceratosaurus's role as an intelligent small predator during the Middle Jurassic
