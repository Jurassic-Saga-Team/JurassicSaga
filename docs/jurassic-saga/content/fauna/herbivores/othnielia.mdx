*Othnielia* is a genus of hypsilophodontid dinosaur featured in **Jurassic Park (1992)**.
In Jurassic Saga, it represents a small, agile herbivore often found darting between vegetation in lightly wooded areas.

---

## Appearance
- Small, lightweight body built for speed and agility
- Narrow head with a beaked mouth suited for cropping vegetation
- Long tail used for balance while running
- In-game size: **3 blocks long** and **1 block tall**

---

## Behavior
- **Neutral**: Will not attack unless provoked
- Retaliates when attacked
- Often flees from larger predators or players when alone
- Moves in small groups, staying alert and skittish

---

## Obtaining
Othnielia does not spawn naturally. To obtain one, you must:
1. Find ![](@jurassic-saga:textures/item/natural/entity/animal/v2/extinct/othnielia/othnielia_fossil_remains) [**Othnielia Remains**]($content/items/fossil) or fossils
2. Use the fossil revival process to create an Othnielia
3. Hatch from a **Basilisk Egg** type egg

---

## Diet
- **Herbivore**: feeds primarily on <img src="https://i.imgur.com/INKBvsq.gif" width={32} height={32} style={{imageRendering: "pixelated"}}/> [**Leaves**](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Leaves)
- Grazes on low vegetation and shrubs

---

## Drops
When defeated, Othnielia may drop:
- ![](@jurassic-saga:textures/item/natural/entity/animal/v2/extinct/othnielia/othnielia_fossil_remains) [**Othnielia Remains**]($content/items/fossil)
- ![](@jurassic-saga:textures/item/natural/entity/animal/v2/extinct/othnielia/othnielia_raw) [**Othnielia Raw Meat**]($content/items/meats)
