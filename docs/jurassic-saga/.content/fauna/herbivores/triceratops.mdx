# Triceratops horridus

*Triceratops* is an extinct genus of herbivorous chasmosaurine ceratopsid dinosaur that lived in North America during the very end of the Cretaceous period.  
It is one of the most iconic dinosaurs, known for its three horns, large frill, and powerful build.

---

## Appearance
- Large, robust ceratopsian with a massive skull
- Three distinctive facial horns: two long brow horns and a shorter nasal horn
- Broad frill extending from the back of the skull
- In-game size: **11 blocks long** and **5 blocks tall**

---

## Behavior
- **Neutral**: Will not attack unless provoked
- Retaliates when attacked
- Often found in small herds, offering mutual protection
- Uses its horns and bulk to defend itself from predators

---

## Obtaining
Triceratops does not spawn naturally. To obtain one, you must:
1. Find ![](@jurassic-saga:textures/item/natural/entity/animal/v1/extinct/triceratops/triceratops_fossil_remains) [**Triceratops Remains**](@jurassic-saga:fossil) or fossils
2. Use the fossil revival process to create a Triceratops
3. Hatch from an **Ostrich Egg** type egg

---

## Diet
- **Herbivore**: feeds primarily on <img src="https://i.imgur.com/INKBvsq.gif" width={32} height={32} style={{imageRendering: "pixelated"}}/> [**Leaves**](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Leaves)
- Grazes on shrubs and low vegetation

---

## Drops
When defeated, Triceratops may drop:  
- ![](@jurassic-saga:textures/item/natural/entity/animal/v1/extinct/triceratops/triceratops_fossil_remains) [**Triceratops Remains**](@jurassic-saga:fossil)
- ![](@jurassic-saga:textures/item/natural/entity/animal/v1/extinct/triceratops/triceratops_raw) [**Triceratops Raw Meat**](@jurassic-saga:meats)

## Trivia
- The achievement "Grant's Favorite" references Dr. Alan Grant from *Jurassic Park*, whose favorite dinosaur was Triceratops, featured in the memorable sick Triceratops scene
- Triceratops was one of the last non-avian dinosaurs to exist before the Cretaceous-Paleogene extinction event
- The name *Triceratops* means "three-horned face"
